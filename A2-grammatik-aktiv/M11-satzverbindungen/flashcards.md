# Module 11 — Flashcards: Satzverbindungen

---

## Type Cards

| Front | Back |
|---|---|
| und/oder/aber/denn/sondern → Position? | **Position 0** (no word order change) |
| deshalb/trotzdem/sonst/dann → Position? | **Position 1** (inversion!) |

---

## Meaning Cards

| Front | Back |
|---|---|
| and (addition) → ? | **und** |
| or (alternative) → ? | **oder** |
| but (contrast) → ? | **aber** |
| because (reason, normal word order) → ? | **denn** |
| but rather (after negation) → ? | **sondern** |
| therefore (result) → ? | **deshalb** |
| nevertheless (unexpected) → ? | **trotzdem** |
| otherwise (warning) → ? | **sonst** |
| then (sequence) → ? | **dann / danach** |

---

## Word Order Cards

| Front | Back |
|---|---|
| ..., aber ich ___ kein Geld. (haben) | ..., aber ich **habe** kein Geld. (normal) |
| Ich bin krank. Deshalb ___ ich zu Hause. (bleiben) | Deshalb **bleibe** ich zu Hause. (inversion!) |
