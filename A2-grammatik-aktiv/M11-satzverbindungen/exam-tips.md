# Module 11 — Exam Tips: Satzverbindungen

> 🎯 **Goethe A2:** Sentence connectors appear in **Schreiben** (connecting ideas in emails) and **Sprechen** (giving reasons, sequences). Using them correctly shows A2-level competence.

---

## How This Grammar Appears

| Section | What to Watch |
|---|---|
| **Schreiben** | Connecting sentences in emails: "Ich komme nicht, **denn** ich bin krank." |
| **Sprechen** | Giving reasons and sequences: "Zuerst... **dann**... **danach**..." |
| **Lesen** | Understanding cause-effect: "Es regnete. **Deshalb** blieb er zu Hause." |

## Key Tips

1. **The Word Order Test**
   * After und/oder/aber/denn/sondern → Subject comes BEFORE verb (normal)
   * After deshalb/trotzdem/sonst/dann → Verb comes BEFORE subject (inversion!)

2. **Schreiben: The Sequence Template**
   * *Zuerst habe ich gefrühstückt. **Dann** bin ich zur Arbeit gefahren. **Danach** habe ich eingekauft.*
   * This template works for any "describe your day" task!

3. **aber vs sondern**
   * Contrast without negation → **aber**: *Teuer, **aber** gut.*
   * Correction after nicht/kein → **sondern**: *Nicht Kaffee, **sondern** Tee.*

> 🇻🇳 *Mẹo: Trong bài Schreiben A2, hãy dùng ít nhất 3 liên từ khác nhau. Ví dụ: "und" + "aber" + "deshalb". Đây là cách đơn giản nhất để nâng điểm.*
