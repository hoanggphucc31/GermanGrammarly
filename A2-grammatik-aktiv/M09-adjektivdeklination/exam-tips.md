# Module 09 — Exam Tips: Adjektivdeklination

> 🎯 **Goethe A2:** Adjective endings appear in Schreiben (describing people/things) and Sprechen (shopping, describing objects). You don't need to be perfect — focus on the most common patterns!

---

## How This Grammar Appears

| Section | What to Watch |
|---|---|
| **Schreiben** | "Describe your new apartment/room." → Use adjectives with articles. |
| **Sprechen** | Shopping role-play: "Ich suche einen **warmen** Pullover." |
| **Lesen** | Product descriptions, apartment ads with adjective+noun phrases. |
| **Hören** | Person descriptions: "Der **große** Mann mit dem **schwarzen** Hut." |

## Key Tips

1. **The Dativ Shortcut**
   * After articles in Dativ (mit **dem**, bei **der**, zu **einem**...), the adjective ending is almost always **-en**.
   * *mit dem nett**en** Mann, in einer schön**en** Stadt*
   * This covers the vast majority of A2 exam situations!

2. **Shopping Template**
   * *Ich suche einen **warmen** Pullover.* (Akk m → -en)
   * *Haben Sie das **rote** Kleid in Größe M?* (Akk n → -e)
   * *Die **blaue** Tasche gefällt mir.* (Nom f → -e)

3. **Don't Panic Strategy**
   * If unsure about the ending, use **-en**. It's correct about 60% of the time!
   * Focus on getting Maskulin Akkusativ right (always -en), since it's the most tested.

4. **Date Expressions**
   * *am erst**en** Mai, am zweit**en** Juni, am nächst**en** Montag*
   * After `am` = Dativ → always **-en**. Easy!

> 🇻🇳 *Mẹo: Nếu không chắc đuôi tính từ, hãy dùng **-en**. Trong đa số trường hợp (Dativ, Akk maskulin, Plural), -en đều đúng. Đây là chiến lược "an toàn" trong bài thi.*
