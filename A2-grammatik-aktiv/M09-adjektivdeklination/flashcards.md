# Module 09 — Flashcards: Adjektivdeklination

---

## Ending Pattern Cards

| Front | Back |
|---|---|
| After der/die/das, Nom → ? | **-e** (der nett**e** Mann) |
| After der/die/das, Akk m → ? | **-en** (den nett**en** Mann) |
| After articles in Dativ → ? | **-en** (mit dem nett**en** Mann) |
| After ein, Nom m → ? | **-er** (ein nett**er** Mann) ⚠️ |
| After ein, Nom/Akk n → ? | **-es** (ein nett**es** Kind) ⚠️ |

|---|---|
| am ___ Mai (1.) | am erst**en** Mai |

| am ___ Freitag (last) | am letzt**en** Freitag |

---

## Key Rules

| Front | Back |
|---|---|
| Dativ adjective ending (after articles)? | Almost always **-en** |
| Why does "ein" need -er/-es? | Because "ein" has no ending → adjective must carry the gender signal. |
| Plural definite, all cases? | Always **-en** |
