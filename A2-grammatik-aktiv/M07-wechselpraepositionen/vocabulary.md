## 2 · Position Verbs (Wo? + Dativ)

| German | Meaning | 🇻🇳 |
|---|---|---|
| **stehen** | to stand | đứng |
| **liegen** | to lie | nằm |
| **sitzen** | to sit | ngồi |
| **hängen** | to hang (state) | treo (trạng thái) |

## 3 · Direction Verbs (Wohin? + Akkusativ)

| German | Meaning | 🇻🇳 |
|---|---|---|
| **stellen** | to place upright | đặt đứng |
| **legen** | to lay flat | đặt nằm |
| **setzen** | to set / sit down | ngồi xuống |
| **hängen** | to hang (action) | treo (hành động) |
| **gehen** | to go | đi |
| **fallen** | to fall | rơi |
| **springen** | to jump | nhảy |

## 4 · Contractions

| Full Form | Short | Case |
|---|---|---|
| in + dem | **im** | Dativ |
| in + das | **ins** | Akkusativ |
| an + dem | **am** | Dativ |
| an + das | **ans** | Akkusativ |
