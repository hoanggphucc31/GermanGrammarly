# Module 01 — Exam Tips: Präteritum (sein & haben)

> 🎯 **Goethe A2:** `war` und `hatte` are used constantly in exams — for apology emails, vacation stories, and spoken descriptions of past events.

---

## How This Grammar Appears

| Section | What to Watch |
|---|---|
| **Lesen** | Recognizing past descriptions: "Das Wetter war schön. Wir hatten ein tolles Hotel." |
| **Schreiben** | Apology emails: "Es tut mir leid, ich war krank und hatte keine Zeit." |
| **Sprechen** | Part 2 (Erzählen): "Letztes Jahr war ich in München. Es war super!" |

## Key Tips

1. **The "Excuse Email" Formula (Schreiben)**
   * Goethe A2 Schreiben often requires you to apologize for missing an event.
   * Master this template:
   * *Es tut mir leid, aber ich **war** krank.*
   * *Ich **hatte** viel Stress auf der Arbeit.*
   * *Ich **hatte** leider keine Zeit.*

2. **Describing Vacations (Sprechen)**
   * When talking about a trip, use `war` for describing conditions:
   * *Das Hotel **war** toll. Das Essen **war** lecker. Die Leute **waren** nett.*

3. **V2 Rule is CRITICAL**
   * ❌ *Letztes Jahr ich war in München.*
   * ✅ *Letztes Jahr **war ich** in München.*
   * Time word in position 1 → Verb in position 2 → Subject in position 3.

4. **Don't overthink it**
   * `war` and `hatte` are the easiest past tense forms in German. No Partizip, no auxiliaries. Just conjugate and put in position 2.

> 🇻🇳 *Mẹo: Trong bài thi Schreiben, hãy bắt đầu câu bằng từ chỉ thời gian + war/hatte. Cách viết này rất tự nhiên và dễ ăn điểm ngữ pháp.*
