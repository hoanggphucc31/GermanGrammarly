# Module 08 — Exam Tips: Lokale Präpositionen

> 🎯 **Goethe A2:** Local prepositions are tested in Sprechen (giving directions, describing routes), Hören (understanding locations), and Schreiben (describing where you live, work, go).

---

## How This Grammar Appears

| Section | What to Watch |
|---|---|
| **Hören** | Understanding locations: "Der Supermarkt ist **in der** Hauptstraße." |
| **Lesen** | Advertisements and announcements with place descriptions. |
| **Schreiben** | Emails about meeting places: "Treffen wir uns **im** Café oder **am** Bahnhof?" |
| **Sprechen** | Part 2: Describing your route, giving directions. |

## Key Tips

1. **The Three-Step Route Formula (Sprechen)**
   * To describe where you go, use this pattern:
   * *Ich komme **von zu Hause**.* (Woher?)
   * *Zuerst gehe ich **zum** Bäcker.* (Wohin?)
   * *Dann bin ich **in der** Schule.* (Wo?)

2. **beim/zum/vom Triple**
   * For services (doctor, bakery, hairdresser), ALWAYS use:
   * **beim** = there right now (Wo?)
   * **zum** = going there (Wohin?)
   * **vom** = coming from there (Woher?)

3. **in vs nach vs zu — The Quick Decision**
   * City/Country (no article) → **nach** (nach Berlin)
   * Country WITH article → **in** (in die Schweiz)
   * Person/Service → **zu** (zum Arzt, zu Lisa)
   * Building/Room/Enclosed place → **in** (ins Kino, in die Schule)

4. **Home Expressions (Memorize!)**
   * at home = **zu Hause** *(NOT in Hause!)*
   * going home = **nach Hause** *(NOT zu Hause!)*

5. **auf for Open Places**
   * The textbook specifically uses `auf` for open/outdoor areas:
   * **auf den** Markt, **auf den** Sportplatz
   * If in doubt between `auf` and `in`: outdoor/open → `auf`. Enclosed rooms → `in`.

> 🇻🇳 *Mẹo: Trong bài thi Sprechen, khi mô tả lộ trình hàng ngày, hãy dùng 3 cụm: "Zuerst gehe ich zum... Dann fahre ich in die... Zum Schluss gehe ich nach Hause." Đây là cách tổ chức bài nói đơn giản nhưng ăn điểm.*
