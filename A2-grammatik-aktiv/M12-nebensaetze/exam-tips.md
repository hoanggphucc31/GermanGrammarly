# Module 12 — Exam Tips: Nebensätze

> 🎯 **Goethe A2:** Nebensätze with weil/dass/wenn appear in **Schreiben** (giving reasons) and **Sprechen** (expressing opinions). This is the #1 grammar structure that separates A2 from A1!

---

## How This Grammar Appears

| Section | What to Watch |
|---|---|
| **Schreiben** | Giving reasons: "Ich komme nicht, **weil** ich krank **bin**." |
| **Sprechen** | Opinions: "Ich finde, **dass** Deutsch interessant **ist**." |
| **Hören** | Understanding conditions: "**Wenn** Sie Fragen **haben**, rufen Sie an." |

## Key Tips

1. **The Verb-at-End Rule**
   * After weil/dass/wenn/ob, the verb goes to the END. Always!
   * With Perfekt: *..., weil ich zu viel gegessen **habe**.* (habe at end)
   * With Modalverb: *..., weil ich arbeiten **muss**.* (muss at end)

2. **wenn vs ob — The Quick Test**
   * Can you replace with "whether"? → **ob**
   * Can you replace with "when/if (condition)"? → **wenn**

3. **Schreiben: The Reason Template**
   * *Ich kann nicht kommen, **weil** ich arbeiten **muss**.*
   * *Ich finde, **dass** das eine gute Idee **ist**.*
   * Use at least one weil-Satz and one dass-Satz per email!

> 🇻🇳 *Mẹo: Verb cuối câu = quy tắc vàng duy nhất cần nhớ cho Nebensatz. Nếu bạn đặt verb đúng vị trí, giám khảo sẽ biết bạn đã nắm cấu trúc A2.*
