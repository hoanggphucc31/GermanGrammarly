# A2 Study Roadmap — Grammatik Aktiv

> **Recommended pace:** 1 module per week (5-6 hours total per week).

---

## Phase 1: Past Tense System (Weeks 1-4) ✅

| Week | Module | Focus | Target After |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **M01** | Präteritum: sein/haben | Can use war/hatte naturally |
| 2 | **M02** | Perfekt | Can tell stories in the past |
| 3 | **M03** | Modal past + Zeitengebrauch | Can explain reasons, mix tenses |
| 4 | **M04** | Reflexive Verben | Can describe daily routines |

## Phase 2: Präpositionen (Weeks 5-8) ✅

| Week | Module | Focus | Target After |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | **M05** | Temporale Präpositionen | Can make appointments, tell schedules |
| 6 | **M06** | Dativ & Akk Prepositions | Can describe transport, gifts, destinations |
| 7 | **M07** | Wechselpräpositionen | Can describe rooms, give directions |
| 8 | **M08** | Lokale Präpositionen | Can give complete location descriptions |

## Phase 3: Adjektive & Vergleichen (Weeks 9-10) ✅

| Week | Module | Focus | Target After |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | **M09** | Adjektivdeklination + Ordinals | Can use adjective endings, express dates |
| 10 | **M10** | Komparativ & Superlativ | Can compare things and express preferences |

## Phase 4: Satzverbindungen & Nebensätze (Weeks 11-12) ✅

| Week | Module | Focus | Target After |
|---|---|---|---|
| 11 | **M11** | Satzverbindungen (Kap. 40-41) | Can connect sentences with und/aber/deshalb/trotzdem |
| 12 | **M12** | Nebensätze (Kap. 42) | Can use weil/dass/wenn/ob with verb at end |

## Phase 5: Wortschatz & Strukturerweiterung (Weeks 13-15) ✅

| Week | Module | Focus | Target After |
|---|---|---|---|
| 13 | **M13** | Lokale Adverbien & Partikeln (Kap. 43-44) | Can use da/dort/dahin, sound natural with denn/mal |
| 14 | **M14** | Wortbildung (Kap. 45-46) | Can decode compound words, predict gender from suffixes |
| 15 | **M15** | Zusammengesetzte Verben & Genusregeln (Kap. 47-48) | Can distinguish sep./insep. verbs, apply gender rules |

---

## Study Tips

1. **Start with `lesson.md`** — Read the grammar explanation carefully.
2. **Do `exercises.md`** — Write answers before checking.
3. **Check `answers.md`** — Understand WHY each answer is correct.
4. **Review `vocabulary.md`** — Learn the key words for this module.
5. **Use `flashcards.md`** — Daily 10-minute review.
6. **Read `exam-tips.md`** — Before any practice test.
