# Module 04 — Exam Tips: Reflexive Verben

> 🎯 **Goethe A2:** Reflexive verbs appear in Sprechen (describing daily routines), Schreiben (emails about your day), and Lesen (understanding someone's routine).

---

## How This Grammar Appears

| Section | What to Watch |
|---|---|
| **Sprechen** | Part 2: "Beschreiben Sie Ihren Tag." You need reflexive verbs for daily routine! |
| **Schreiben** | Writing about what you did today. |
| **Lesen** | Reading about someone's morning or evening routine. |

## Key Tips

1. **Morning Routine Template for Sprechen**
   * Memorize this sequence:
   * *Zuerst **stehe** ich auf.*
   * *Dann **dusche** ich **mich**.*
   * *Danach **ziehe** ich **mich** **an** und **kämme** **mir** die Haare.*
   * *Zum Schluss frühstücke ich.*

2. **"sich freuen" in Schreiben**
   * In invitation-response emails, you often need to express happiness:
   * *Ich **freue mich** auf die Party!*
   * *Ich **freue mich** auf deinen Besuch!*

3. **Akk/Dat Trap**
   * The examiners know this is a common mistake:
   * ❌ *Ich wasche **mich** die Hände.* (WRONG)
   * ✅ *Ich wasche **mir** die Hände.* (RIGHT — "die Hände" is the Akk object)
   * If unsure, avoid the body part version entirely and just say *Ich wasche mich.*

4. **Perfekt with Reflexive Verbs**
   * The reflexive pronoun stays the same in Perfekt:
   * *Ich **habe** **mich** geduscht.*
   * *Er **hat** **sich** gefreut.*
   * Don't forget: reflexive verbs use **haben** in Perfekt (not sein)!

> 🇻🇳 *Mẹo: Trong phần Sprechen, khi mô tả ngày thường (Tagesablauf), hãy chuẩn bị sẵn 4-5 câu với động từ phản thân. Giám khảo rất ấn tượng khi bạn dùng đúng "sich duschen", "sich anziehen" — nó chứng tỏ bạn đã vượt qua trình độ A1.*
