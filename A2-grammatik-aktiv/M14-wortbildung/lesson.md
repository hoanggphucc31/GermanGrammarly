## Part 1: Komposita — Compound Words (Kapitel 45)

### 1.1 How German Builds Words

German creates new words by **combining** existing words:

| Word 1 | + | Word 2 | = | Kompositum | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kinder | + | Arzt | = | **Kinderarzt** | children's doctor |
| Arzt | + | Kinder | = | **Arztkinder** | doctor's children |
| Haus | + | Tür | = | **Haustür** | front door |
| Schlaf | + | Zimmer | = | **Schlafzimmer** | bedroom |

> 🎯 **The Last Word Rule:** The LAST word determines the meaning AND the gender!
> * **der** Kinder**arzt** (der Arzt → maskulin)
> * **die** Haus**tür** (die Tür → feminin)

### 1.2 Common Patterns

| Pattern | Examples |
|---|---|
| Noun + Noun | **Busfahrer** (bus driver), **Stadtplan** (city map) |
| Verb stem + Noun | **Schlafzimmer** (sleeping room), **Fahrkarte** (ticket) |
| Adjective + Noun | **Großstadt** (big city), **Schnellzug** (express train) |

### 1.3 Connecting Elements (Fugenelemente)

Sometimes a connecting letter appears between the two words:

| Element | Example |
|---|---|
| **-s-** | Arbeit**s**platz, Geburtstag**s**kind |
| **-n-** | Blume**n**laden, Straße**n**bahn |
| **-er-** | Kind**er**arzt, Büch**er**regal |
| (none) | Haustür, Stadtplan |

> 💡 There's no fixed rule — you learn them with practice. The most common is **-s-**.

## Part 2: Suffixes — Making New Words (Kapitel 46)

### 2.1 Diminutive: -chen / -lein (always Neutrum!)

| Base Word | + Suffix | Result | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| die Katze | + -chen | **das** Kätz**chen** | kitten |
| der Hund | + -chen | **das** Hünd**chen** | little dog |
| die Blume | + -lein | **das** Blüm**lein** | little flower |
| das Brot | + -chen | **das** Bröt**chen** | bread roll |

> 🎯 **Always Neutrum + Umlaut!** -chen/-lein makes ANY word das, and usually adds Umlaut (a→ä, o→ö, u→ü).

### 2.2 Person Suffixes: -er (m) / -in (f)

| Action | Male | Female |
|---|---|---|
| lehren (teach) | **der** Lehr**er** | **die** Lehr**er****in** |
| arbeiten (work) | **der** Arbeit**er** | **die** Arbeit**er****in** |
| fahren (drive) | **der** Fahr**er** | **die** Fahr**er****in** |
| München | **der** Münch(e)n**er** | **die** Münch(e)n**erin** |

### 2.3 Noun from Verb: -ung (always Feminin!)

| Verb | + -ung | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **wohnen** | Wohn**ung** | **die** Wohnung (apartment) |
| **üben** | Üb**ung** | **die** Übung (exercise) |
| **erfahren** | Erfahr**ung** | **die** Erfahrung (experience) |
| **bilden** | Bild**ung** | **die** Bildung (education) |

> 🎯 **Always Feminin!** Every -ung word is die.

### 2.4 Gender Shortcuts from Suffixes

| Suffix | Gender | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **-chen, -lein** | das | Mädchen, Brötchen, Häuschen |
| **-ung** | die | Wohnung, Übung, Zeitung |
| **-er** (person) | der | Lehrer, Fahrer, Arbeiter |
| **-in** (female) | die | Lehrerin, Fahrerin |
| **-heit, -keit** | die | Freiheit, Möglichkeit |

## 3 · Common Mistakes

| # | ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **die** Mädchen | **das** Mädchen | -chen = always das! |
| 2 | **der** Wohnung | **die** Wohnung | -ung = always die! |
| 3 | Arzt + Kinder = Kinderarzt? | Depends on meaning! | Kinderarzt ≠ Arztkinder |
