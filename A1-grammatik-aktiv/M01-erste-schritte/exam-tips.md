# Module 01 — Exam Tips: Personalpronomen

> 🎯 **Goethe A1:** Pronouns appear in ALL exam sections.

---

## How Pronouns Appear in the Exam

| Section | What to Watch |
|---|---|
| **Lesen** | Texts use *er/sie/es* to refer back to people/things — you must know who/what is meant |
| **Hören** | Speakers use *sie/Sie* — decide: she, they, or formal you? |
| **Schreiben** | Use pronouns to avoid repeating names in your message |
| **Sprechen** | Understand when examiner says *Sie* (formal) vs role-play *du* |

## Key Tips

1. **sie/sie/Sie trap:** Always check capital letter + verb form
2. **das Mädchen → es:** Grammar gender wins over natural gender
3. **Things have gender:** *der Kaffee → er, die Milch → sie, das Buch → es*
4. **In the exam, always use Sie** with the examiner

> 🇻🇳 *Mẹo: Khi đọc/nghe bài thi, gặp "sie" → nhìn động từ (số ít = cô ấy, số nhiều = họ, viết hoa = quý vị).*
