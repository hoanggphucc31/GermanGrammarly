## 1 · The 9 Personal Pronouns (Die 9 Personalpronomen)

```
╔═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  Singular                    ║  Plural                       ║
╠══════════════════════════════╬═══════════════════════════════╣
║  ich    = I                  ║  wir   = we                   ║
║  du     = you (informal)     ║  ihr   = you all (informal)   ║
║  er     = he / it (🔵 der)   ║  sie   = they                 ║
║  sie    = she / it (🔴 die)  ║  Sie   = you (formal) ⚠️      ║
║  es     = it (🟢 das)        ║                               ║
╚══════════════════════════════╩═══════════════════════════════╝
```

> 💡 **ich** is always lowercase (except at the start of a sentence). **Sie** (formal) is always capitalized!

> 🇻🇳 *"ich" luôn viết thường (trừ đầu câu). "Sie" (trang trọng) luôn viết HOA.*

---

## 2 · Pronouns for People (Pronomen für Personen)

This is the most intuitive use — pronouns replace **people's names** to avoid repetition.

| Person | Pronoun | Example |
|---|---|---|
| a man (1 male person) | **er** | **Der Mann** ist nett. → **Er** ist nett. |
| a woman (1 female person) | **sie** | **Die Frau** ist hier. → **Sie** ist hier. |
| a child (neutral) | **es** | **Das Kind** spielt. → **Es** spielt. |
| a girl ⚠️ | **es** | **Das Mädchen** lernt. → **Es** lernt. |
| a group of people | **sie** (plural) | **Die Leute** kommen. → **Sie** kommen. |

> ⚠️ **das Mädchen** (girl) uses **es** — NOT *sie*! Because *Mädchen* ends in *-chen*, which is always Neutral (das). Grammar gender wins over natural gender!

> 🇻🇳 *"das Mädchen" (cô gái) dùng "es" chứ không phải "sie" — vì đuôi -chen luôn là giống trung (das).*

---

## 3 · Pronouns for Things (Pronomen für Sachen)

This is the part most beginners miss. In German, **every noun has a gender** — and the pronoun must match the **grammatical gender**, not the thing itself.

### The Rule

| Article | Gender | Pronoun | 🇻🇳 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **der** (Maskulin) | 🔵 | → **er** | "anh ấy" cho đồ vật nam |
| **die** (Feminin) | 🔴 | → **sie** | "cô ấy" cho đồ vật nữ |
| **das** (Neutral) | 🟢 | → **es** | "nó" cho đồ vật trung |
| **die** (Plural) | 🔵🔴🟢 | → **sie** (plural) | "chúng nó" |

### Examples with Everyday Objects

| Noun | Gender | Sentence | With Pronoun |
|---|---|---|---|
| **der** Löffel (spoon) | 🔵 | Der Löffel ist alt. | → **Er** ist alt. |
| **die** Gabel (fork) | 🔴 | Die Gabel ist neu. | → **Sie** ist neu. |
| **das** Messer (knife) | 🟢 | Das Messer ist scharf. | → **Es** ist scharf. |
| **der** Tisch (table) | 🔵 | Der Tisch ist groß. | → **Er** ist groß. |
| **die** Lampe (lamp) | 🔴 | Die Lampe ist kaputt. | → **Sie** ist kaputt. |
| **das** Buch (book) | 🟢 | Das Buch ist interessant. | → **Es** ist interessant. |
| **der** Computer | 🔵 | Der Computer ist neu. | → **Er** ist neu. |
| **die** Tasche (bag) | 🔴 | Die Tasche ist rot. | → **Sie** ist rot. |
| **das** Handy (phone) | 🟢 | Das Handy ist teuer. | → **Es** ist teuer. |

> 💡 **Memory trick:** In German, a spoon is "he" (er), a fork is "she" (sie), and a knife is "it" (es). Sounds strange, but this is how German works!

> 🇻🇳 *Trong tiếng Đức, cái thìa = "anh ấy" (er), cái nĩa = "cô ấy" (sie), con dao = "nó" (es). Lạ nhưng đúng!*

### Plural: Always *sie*

| Plural Noun | Pronoun | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **die** Löffel (spoons) | **sie** | Die Löffel sind alt. → **Sie** sind alt. |
| **die** Bücher (books) | **sie** | Die Bücher sind neu. → **Sie** sind neu. |
| **die** Kinder (children) | **sie** | Die Kinder spielen. → **Sie** spielen. |

> 💡 Plural → always **die** → always **sie**. No exceptions!

## 4 · sie / sie / Sie — The Triple Trap

The most confusing part of German pronouns: **three different words that look (almost) the same**.

### How to Tell Them Apart

| Written | Meaning | Verb Form | How to Identify |
|---|---|---|---|
| **sie** (lowercase) | **she** | singular: *ist, hat, kommt* | lowercase + singular verb |
| **sie** (lowercase) | **they** | plural: *sind, haben, kommen* | lowercase + plural verb |
| **Sie** (CAPITAL) | **you (formal)** | plural: *sind, haben, kommen* | Capital S (always!) |

### Decision Flowchart

```
Is "S" capitalized?
├── YES → formal "you" (Sie)
└── NO → look at the verb:
    ├── singular verb (ist, hat, kommt) → "she" (sie)
    └── plural verb (sind, haben, kommen) → "they" (sie)
```

### Practice — What Does *sie/Sie* Mean Here?

| Sentence | sie = ? | How I Know |
|---|---|---|
| **Sie** kommen morgen. | formal you **OR** they | Capital S = formal you; but at sentence start, could be either → use context! |
| Maria ist nett. **sie** kommt aus Berlin. | she | lowercase + singular verb *kommt* |
| Die Kinder spielen. **sie** sind laut. | they | lowercase + plural verb *sind* |
| Herr Müller, können **Sie** mir helfen? | formal you | Capital S + addressing someone formally |

> ⚠️ At the **start of a sentence**, *sie* and *Sie* look the same → you MUST use context and verb form!

> 🇻🇳 *3 chữ "sie" cùng viết giống nhau! Cách phân biệt: (1) viết hoa = quý vị, (2) động từ số ít = cô ấy, (3) động từ số nhiều = họ.*

## 5 · du or Sie? — Choosing the Right Register

| Use **du** (+ **ihr** for plural) | Use **Sie** |
|---|---|
| Friends, family | Strangers, adults you don't know |
| Children | Your boss, colleagues (initially) |
| Classmates, young people | Doctors, officials, police |
| Social media, informal settings | Shops, restaurants, formal settings |
| When someone says *"Wir können uns duzen"* → switch to du! | Goethe exam → **always Sie with examiners** |

> 🎯 **Goethe A1 Tip:** In the exam, always use **Sie** with the examiner. Use **du** only when role-playing with another candidate.

## 7 · Common Mistakes (Häufige Fehler)

| # | ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Das Mädchen ist nett. → **Sie** ist nett. | → **Es** ist nett. | *Mädchen* = das (Neutral) → es |
| 2 | Der Kaffee ist gut. → **Es** ist gut. | → **Er** ist gut. | *Kaffee* = der (Maskulin) → er |
| 3 | Die Tasche ist rot. → **Er** ist rot. | → **Sie** ist rot. | *Tasche* = die (Feminin) → sie |
| 4 | *sie* kommen morgen. (= formal you?) | **Sie** kommen morgen. | Formal you = always capital **S** |
| 5 | Die Bücher sind neu. → **Es** ist neu. | → **Sie** sind neu. | Plural = sie + plural verb |
| 6 | *Ich* bin Student. (mid-sentence) | **ich** bin Student. | *ich* is lowercase (except sentence start) |

## 9 · Learning Strategies (Lerntipps)

### 🎯 Step 1: Always Learn Nouns WITH Their Article
Don't learn *Tisch = table*. Learn ***der** Tisch = table* → then you automatically know → **er**.

### 🔁 Step 2: Substitution Drill
Point at objects around you: *Der Laptop → er. Die Tasse → sie. Das Bild → es.*

### 📝 Step 3: sie/sie/Sie Practice
Read German texts and mark every *sie/Sie*. Decide: she, they, or formal you?
