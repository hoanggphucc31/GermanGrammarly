# Module 07 — Exam Tips: Nomen, Artikel & Plural

> 🎯 **Goethe A1:** Articles and plurals appear in ALL exam sections — especially Lesen and Schreiben.

---

## How This Grammar Appears

| Section | What to Watch |
|---|---|
| **Lesen** | Recognize articles in notices, signs, forms |
| **Schreiben** | Use correct articles in messages and form-filling |
| **Hören** | Listen for gender clues in definite articles |
| **Sprechen** | Name objects and people with correct articles |

## Key Tips

1. **Learn every noun with its article** — this is the #1 long-term strategy
2. **-ung = die, -chen = das** — these two rules alone cover hundreds of nouns
3. **Compound nouns: last word wins** — no need to memorize compounds separately
4. **No indefinite plural** — *Das sind Bücher* (not ~~eine Bücher~~)
5. **Professions without adjective → no article** — *Ich bin Lehrer*

## Speed Drill

| Fill in | Answer |
|---|---|
| ___ Wohnung | **die** (-ung) |
| ___ Brötchen | **das** (-chen) |
| der Stuhl → plural | die **Stühle** |
| Ich bin ___ Ingenieur. | **—** (no article) |

> 🇻🇳 *Trong thi: luôn nhớ article khi viết. -ung = die, -chen = das. Nghề nghiệp không tính từ → bỏ article.*
