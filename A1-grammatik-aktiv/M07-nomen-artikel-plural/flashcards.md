# Module 07 — Flashcards: Nomen, Artikel & Plural

---

## Core Gender Rules

| Front | Back |
|---|---|
| -ung → ? | 🔴 **die** (die Wohnung) |
| -chen → ? | 🟢 **das** (das Mädchen) |
| -heit/-keit → ? | 🔴 **die** (die Freiheit) |
| -tion → ? | 🔴 **die** (die Information) |
| Days of the week → ? | 🔵 **der** (der Montag) |
| Compound noun gender? | Last word decides! |

---

## Plural Patterns

| Front | Back |
|---|---|
| der Tisch → plural | die **Tische** (-e) |
| die Lampe → plural | die **Lampen** (-(e)n) |
| das Kind → plural | die **Kinder** (-er) |
| das Auto → plural | die **Autos** (-s) |
| der Lehrer → plural | die **Lehrer** (-∅) |
| das Buch → plural | die **Bücher** (-er + Umlaut) |

---

## Articles

| Front | Back |
|---|---|
| specific noun → ? | **der/die/das** (definite) |
| new/unknown noun → ? | **ein/eine/ein** (indefinite) |
| plural indefinite? | **no article** (Das sind Bücher.) |
| profession (no adj)? | **no article** (Ich bin Lehrer.) |
