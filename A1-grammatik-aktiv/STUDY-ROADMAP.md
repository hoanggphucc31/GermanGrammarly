## How This Curriculum Is Built

This curriculum follows `Grammatik aktiv A1-B1` in a structured A1 path.

Current design logic:

1. Start with pronouns and subject reference.
2. Learn present-tense verb endings.
3. Learn key irregular verbs and vowel change.
4. Expand into modal verbs, sentence structure, nouns, cases, prepositions, and exam application.

Current planned sequence from the curriculum:

| Phase | Weeks | Focus | Modules |
|---|---|---|---|
| Phase 1 | Weeks 1-2 | Foundation grammar from book chapters 1-4 | M01-M03 |
| Phase 2 | Weeks 3-4 | Modal verbs, separable verbs, sentence structure | M04-M06 |
| Phase 3 | Weeks 5-6 | Nouns, negation, cases, possessives | M07-M10 |
| Phase 4 | Weeks 7-8 | Prepositions, time, Perfekt intro, exam integration | M11-M12 |

For the current module mapping, see:
- [README.md](README.md)
- [CURRICULUM-MAP.md](CURRICULUM-MAP.md)

## Phase 1 Roadmap

Phase 1 is already available and should be studied in order.

### M01: Personalpronomen

**Book alignment:** Kapitel 1

**What you learn:**
- 9 personal pronouns
- pronouns for people and things
- grammatical gender to pronoun mapping
- `sie / sie / Sie`
- choosing `du` vs `Sie`

**Files:**
- [M01 lesson](M01-erste-schritte/lesson.md)
- [M01 vocabulary](M01-erste-schritte/vocabulary.md)
- [M01 flashcards](M01-erste-schritte/flashcards.md)
- [M01 exercises](M01-erste-schritte/exercises.md)
- [M01 answers](M01-erste-schritte/answers.md)
- [M01 exam tips](M01-erste-schritte/exam-tips.md)

**Study goal:**
- You can replace nouns correctly with `er`, `sie`, `es`, `sie`, `Sie`.

**Minimum output before moving on:**
- You can explain why `der Löffel -> er`, `die Gabel -> sie`, `das Messer -> es`.
- You can distinguish `sie = she`, `sie = they`, and `Sie = formal you`.

### M02: Konjugation Präsens

**Book alignment:** Kapitel 2

**What you learn:**
- the regular present-tense ending pattern
- model verb conjugation
- `kommen`, `wohnen`, `lernen`, `machen`, `arbeiten`, `heißen`
- special spelling/pronunciation cases
- basic sentence building with a conjugated verb

**Files:**
- [M02 lesson](M02-pronomen-sein/lesson.md)
- [M02 vocabulary](M02-pronomen-sein/vocabulary.md)
- [M02 flashcards](M02-pronomen-sein/flashcards.md)
- [M02 exercises](M02-pronomen-sein/exercises.md)
- [M02 answers](M02-pronomen-sein/answers.md)
- [M02 exam tips](M02-pronomen-sein/exam-tips.md)

**Study goal:**
- You can conjugate regular verbs confidently in the present tense.

**Minimum output before moving on:**
- You know `-e / -st / -t / -en / -t / -en` without looking.
- You can write simple sentences such as `Ich wohne in Berlin.` and `Woher kommst du?`

### M03: Sein, haben, besondere Verben & Vokalwechsel

**Book alignment:** Kapitel 3-4

**What you learn:**
- `sein`
- `haben`
- `möchten`, `wissen`, `tun`
- `sein` vs `haben`
- vowel-changing verbs such as `sprechen`, `essen`, `lesen`, `sehen`, `fahren`, `schlafen`, `nehmen`

**Files:**
- [M03 lesson](M03-haben-possessiv/lesson.md)
- [M03 vocabulary](M03-haben-possessiv/vocabulary.md)
- [M03 flashcards](M03-haben-possessiv/flashcards.md)
- [M03 exercises](M03-haben-possessiv/exercises.md)
- [M03 answers](M03-haben-possessiv/answers.md)
- [M03 exam tips](M03-haben-possessiv/exam-tips.md)

**Study goal:**
- You can use the most important early irregular verbs correctly.

**Minimum output before moving on:**
- You can produce `ich bin`, `du bist`, `er ist`, `ich habe`, `du hast`, `er hat` automatically.
- You know that vowel change happens mainly in `du` and `er/sie/es` forms.

## Phase 2 Roadmap

Phase 2 is now available and covers modal verbs, separable verbs, imperative, and sentence structure.

### M04: Modalverben

**Book alignment:** Kapitel 5-6

**What you learn:**
- 6 modal verbs: können, müssen, dürfen, wollen, sollen, möchten
- modal conjugation (ich = er, no ending!)
- sentence frame: modal in position 2, infinitive at end
- meaning distinctions: können vs dürfen, müssen vs sollen, wollen vs möchten
- signs and rules

**Files:**
- [M04 lesson](M04-modalverben/lesson.md)
- [M04 vocabulary](M04-modalverben/vocabulary.md)
- [M04 flashcards](M04-modalverben/flashcards.md)
- [M04 exercises](M04-modalverben/exercises.md)
- [M04 answers](M04-modalverben/answers.md)
- [M04 exam tips](M04-modalverben/exam-tips.md)

**Study goal:**
- You can express ability, permission, obligation, and wishes using modals.

**Minimum output before moving on:**
- You can conjugate all 6 modals from memory.
- You can build a sentence with the modal in pos. 2 and infinitive at the end.
- You know that "must not" = darf nicht (NOT muss nicht).

### M05: Trennbare Verben & Imperativ

**Book alignment:** Kapitel 7-8

**What you learn:**
- separable verbs and their prefixes
- prefix goes to the end in present tense
- with modals: prefix stays attached
- imperative: du / ihr / Sie forms
- irregular imperatives (Sprich! Nimm! Lies!)
- polite softeners: bitte, doch, mal

**Files:**
- [M05 lesson](M05-trennbar-imperativ/lesson.md)
- [M05 vocabulary](M05-trennbar-imperativ/vocabulary.md)
- [M05 flashcards](M05-trennbar-imperativ/flashcards.md)
- [M05 exercises](M05-trennbar-imperativ/exercises.md)
- [M05 answers](M05-trennbar-imperativ/answers.md)
- [M05 exam tips](M05-trennbar-imperativ/exam-tips.md)

**Study goal:**
- You can use separable verbs in present tense and with modals.
- You can give commands in du/ihr/Sie forms.

**Minimum output before moving on:**
- You can produce: *Ich stehe um 7 Uhr auf.* / *Ich muss um 7 Uhr aufstehen.*
- You know: Sprich! Nimm! Lies! (e→i in imperative) but Fahr! (NO Umlaut).

### M06: Fragen & Wortstellung

**Book alignment:** Kapitel 9-12

**What you learn:**
- question words: wer, was, wo, woher, wohin, wann, warum, wie...
- W-question structure
- yes/no question inversion
- verb position in statements (V2 rule)
- position 1 flexibility
- sentence frame awareness
- doch (contradicts negative questions)

**Files:**
- [M06 lesson](M06-fragen-wortstellung/lesson.md)
- [M06 vocabulary](M06-fragen-wortstellung/vocabulary.md)
- [M06 flashcards](M06-fragen-wortstellung/flashcards.md)
- [M06 exercises](M06-fragen-wortstellung/exercises.md)
- [M06 answers](M06-fragen-wortstellung/answers.md)
- [M06 exam tips](M06-fragen-wortstellung/exam-tips.md)

**Study goal:**
- You can ask and answer W-questions and yes/no questions.
- You understand verb position in all basic sentence types.

**Minimum output before moving on:**
- You know: wo = static, woher = from, wohin = to.
- You can transform a statement into a W-question and a yes/no question.
- You know that verb is ALWAYS in position 2 in statements.

## Recommended Daily Workflow

Use this 3-day cycle for each module.

### Day 1: Input

1. Read `lesson.md` once for overview.
2. Read it again slowly and mark difficult points.
3. Review `vocabulary.md`.
4. Review `flashcards.md` once.

### Day 2: Controlled Practice

1. Read key sections of `lesson.md` again.
2. Do `exercises.md` without checking answers.
3. Mark any items you are unsure about.
4. Check with `answers.md`.

### Day 3: Consolidation

1. Read `exam-tips.md`.
2. Redo incorrect exercise items.
3. Say 5-10 model sentences aloud.
4. Review `flashcards.md` again.
5. Write a short personal mini-practice using the grammar.

## Recommended Review System

To avoid forgetting earlier grammar, use this review pattern:

### After finishing M01

- review pronouns once the next day
- test yourself on noun -> pronoun substitution

### After finishing M02

- review M01 and M02 together
- practice pronoun + verb ending combinations

### After finishing M03

- review M01-M03 as one block
- compare:
  - subject pronoun
  - present-tense ending
  - irregular form
  - vowel-change form

### After finishing M04

- review M01-M04 together
- practice: modal + infinitive at end sentences
- compare: können vs dürfen, müssen vs sollen

### After finishing M05

- review separable verb sentences with and without modals
- practice imperative forms aloud

### After finishing M06

- review M01-M06 as one block
- transform statements into W-questions and yes/no questions
- practice wo/woher/wohin triple

### Weekly review rule

At least once per week:

1. revisit one old `flashcards.md`
2. redo 5-10 old exercise items
3. write 5 grammar sentences without looking

## Practical Study Targets by Stage

### Target after M01

You should be able to:

- identify all 9 pronouns
- replace nouns correctly with pronouns
- understand basic reference in reading/listening

### Target after M02

You should be able to:

- conjugate regular verbs in the present tense
- ask and answer simple personal questions
- build basic present-tense statements and questions

### Target after M03

You should be able to:

- use `sein` and `haben` automatically in common A1 situations
- use `möchten` politely
- recognize common irregular patterns
- understand and produce basic vowel-change verbs

### Target after M04

You should be able to:

- use all 6 modal verbs in present tense
- build sentences with modal + infinitive at end
- distinguish können/dürfen, müssen/sollen, wollen/möchten
- understand signs and rules using modals

### Target after M05

You should be able to:

- use common separable verbs in present tense
- combine separable verbs with modals
- give polite commands in du/ihr/Sie forms

### Target after M06

You should be able to:

- ask W-questions and yes/no questions correctly
- apply V2 rule in statements
- use wo/woher/wohin correctly
- use doch to contradict negative questions

## If You Are Self-Studying

Recommended pace:

- light pace: 1 module every 7-10 days
- normal pace: 1 module every 4-6 days
- intensive pace: 1 module every 2-3 days

If you are studying alone, do this for every module:

1. read aloud every example sentence
2. create 5 personal sentences of your own
3. review flashcards twice, not once
4. revisit the same module after 3 days
