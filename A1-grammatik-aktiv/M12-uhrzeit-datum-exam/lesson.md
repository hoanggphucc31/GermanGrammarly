## 1 · Telling Time (Die Uhrzeit)

There are two ways to tell time in German: **official** (formal) and **unofficial** (informal). 

### 1.1 Official Time (formell)

Used in train stations, TV, and official announcements. Uses the 24-hour clock.

* Structure: **Hour + Uhr + Minutes**
* 14:30 → Vierzehn Uhr dreißig
* 08:15 → Acht Uhr fünfzehn
* 20:45 → Zwanzig Uhr fünfundvierzig

### 1.2 Unofficial Time (informell)

Used in daily conversation. Uses the 12-hour clock. Key words:
* **vor** = before (to)
* **nach** = after (past)
* **halb** = half (half *to* the next hour!)
* **Viertel** = quarter

| Time | German (informal) | Meaning | 🇻🇳 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 08:00 | Es ist acht (Uhr). | It's eight. | 8 giờ |
| 08:05 | Es ist **fünf nach** acht. | 5 past 8. | 8 giờ 5 |
| 08:15 | Es ist **Viertel nach** acht. | Quarter past 8. | 8 giờ 15 |
| 08:20 | Es ist **zwanzig nach** acht. | 20 past 8. | 8 giờ 20 |
| 08:30 | Es ist **halb neun**. | Half *to* 9. | 8 rưỡi (chú ý: halb 9 = 8:30!) |
| 08:45 | Es ist **Viertel vor** neun. | Quarter to 9. | 9 giờ kém 15 |
| 08:50 | Es ist **zehn vor** neun. | 10 to 9. | 9 giờ kém 10 |

> ⚠️ **Attention with "halb"**: In German, `halb neun` means 8:30 (half an hour *before* 9), not 9:30!

## 2 · Days, Months, Dates (Tage und Monate)

### 2.1 Prepositions for Time

* **um** + Time (um 8 Uhr / um Viertel nach acht)
* **am** + Days/Parts of the day (am Montag / am Morgen / am Wochenende)
* **im** + Months/Seasons (im Januar / im Sommer)

### 2.2 Days of the Week (Wochentage)

* Montag, Dienstag, Mittwoch, Donnerstag, Freitag, Samstag, Sonntag.
* All days are **Maskulin (der)**.

### 2.3 Months (Monate)

* Januar, Februar, März, April, Mai, Juni, Juli, August, September, Oktober, November, Dezember.
* All months are **Maskulin (der)**.

### 2.4 Writing Dates (Datum)

In emails or letters (common in A1 Schreiben), you use the ordinal number + dot.

* 01.05. → am ersten Mai / am 1. Mai
* 15.08. → am fünfzehnten August / am 15. August
* 20.12. → am zwanzigsten Dezember / am 20. Dezember

## 3 · Appointments (Termine)

Asking for the time or making an appointment:

> **A:** **Wie spät ist es?** (What time is it?)
> **B:** Es ist Viertel nach zehn. (10:15)

> **A:** **Wann** kommst du? (When are you coming?)
> **B:** Ich komme **um** 14 Uhr. (At 2 PM)

> **A:** Haben Sie **am** Montag Zeit? (Do you have time on Monday?)
> **B:** Nein, aber **am** Dienstag **um** 9 Uhr passt es gut. (No, but Tuesday at 9 works well.)

## 4 · Perfekt: Past Tense Preview (Recognition Only)

> ⚠️ **Supplementary Note:** The full rules for the past tense (Perfekt) start in *Kapitel 23* (A2 level). However, you will encounter basic Perfekt sentences in the A1 reading and listening exams. **You only need to recognize them!**

The Perfekt is used to talk about the past in conversation. 
Structure: **haben or sein (Pos. 2) + ... + Partizip II (End)**

| Present | Past (Perfekt) | Meaning | 🇻🇳 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ich **mache** Pizza. | Ich **habe** Pizza **gemacht**. | I made pizza. | Tôi đã làm pizza. |
| Er **kauft** ein Auto. | Er **hat** ein Auto **gekauft**. | He bought a car. | Anh ấy đã mua xe hơi. |
| Wir **gehen** ins Kino. | Wir **sind** ins Kino **gegangen**.| We went to the cinema. | Chúng tôi đã đi xem phim. |

**Common Partizip II forms to recognize:**
* gemacht (made/did)
* gekauft (bought)
* gelernt (learned)
* gegessen (ate)
* getrunken (drank)
* geschrieben (wrote)
* gelesen (read)
* gekommen (came) - with *sein*
* gefahren (drove/went) - with *sein*

## 5 · Goethe A1 Exam Overview

The exam has 4 parts:

1. **Hören (Listening) - 20 min:**
   * Short announcements, radio, conversations.
   * Focus on numbers, times, days, places.
2. **Lesen (Reading) - 25 min:**
   * Short notes, emails, signs.
   * Focus on vocabulary, cases, true/false.
3. **Schreiben (Writing) - 20 min:**
   * Fill in a form (Formular).
   * Write a short email (e.g., apologizing, inviting, making an appointment).
4. **Sprechen (Speaking) - 15 min:**
   * Introduce yourself (Name, Alter, Land, Wohnort, Sprachen, Beruf, Hobby).
   * Ask and answer questions using word cards (W-Fragen).
   * Make requests using picture cards (Imperativ / möchten).

## 6 · A1 Grammar Final Review (Checklist)

Can you do the following?

- [ ] **M01-M03:** Conjugate verbs in present tense (ich lerne, du lernst, er lernt). Know *sein* and *haben*.
- [ ] **M04:** Use Modal verbs correctly (müssen, können, wollen...). Verb goes to the END.
- [ ] **M05:** Use Separable verbs (Ich stehe um 7 Uhr auf).
- [ ] **M06:** Ask questions (W-Fragen: Woher, Wohin, Was, Wer) and Yes/No questions.
- [ ] **M07-M08:** Use definite/indefinite articles and negate with *kein/nicht*. Form basic plurals.
- [ ] **M09-M11:** Understand the Case System.
   * **Nominativ:** Subject
   * **Akkusativ:** Direct Object (only *der* changes to *den*)
   * **Dativ:** Indirect Object / Receiver (m/n=dem, f=der, pl=den+n)
   * Know verbs that need Dativ (helfen, danken, gehören...)
