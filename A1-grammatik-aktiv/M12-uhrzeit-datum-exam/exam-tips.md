# Module 12 — Exam Tips: Goethe A1 Final Preparation

> 🎯 **Goethe A1:** This is it! The final checklist before you take the exam.

---

## General Exam Strategy

1. **Read the instructions carefully!** Look for words like `ankreuzen` (tick), `richtig` (true), `falsch` (false).
2. **Watch the time.** Don't spend too long on one question. If you don't know the answer in the Lesen part, guess and move on.
3. **In Hören:** You hear parts 1 and 3 TWICE. You hear part 2 ONLY ONCE. 

## The 4 Sections

### 1. Hören (Listening)
* **Numbers & Time:** They will test you on times, prices, and telephone numbers. Pay attention to `halb neun` (8:30) vs `neun Uhr dreißig` (9:30).
* **Places:** Listen for where people are going (ins Kino, zum Arzt).

### 2. Lesen (Reading)
* **True/False:** Read the text first, then the question. Does the text say exactly what the question says?
* **Emails:** Pay attention to who is writing to whom. (Liebe Anna vs Lieber Herr Müller).

### 3. Schreiben (Writing)
* **Formular:** You must fill in missing details on a form (Name, Alter, Adresse, Beruf). Very easy points!
* **Email:** You must write 30 words based on 3 bullet points. 
   * Always write a greeting (Hallo / Sehr geehrte(r)...)
   * Write one simple sentence per bullet point. Keep it simple!
   * Write a sign-off (Viele Grüße).

### 4. Sprechen (Speaking)
* **Part 1 (Introduce yourself):** Memorize your introduction! (Name, Alter, Land, Wohnort, Sprachen, Beruf, Hobby). Spell your name and say your phone number.
* **Part 2 (W-Fragen):** You draw a card with a word. Ask a W-Question. (e.g., Card: "Apfel" → "Was kostet der Apfel?").
* **Part 3 (Requests):** You draw a picture. Make a request using Imperativ or "Können Sie...". (e.g., Picture of a window → "Machen Sie bitte das Fenster auf!").

## Final Golden Rules

* Keep sentences short and simple.
* Use the grammar you know (V2 word order, basic Akk/Dat). Don't try to be too complicated.
* Speak clearly and loudly.
* **Viel Erfolg!** (Good luck!)
