# Module 02 — Exam Tips: Konjugation Präsens

> 🎯 **Goethe A1:** Correct verb endings are tested in every section.

---

## How Conjugation Appears in the Exam

| Section | What to Watch |
|---|---|
| **Lesen** | Verb endings reveal WHO is doing the action |
| **Hören** | Listen for endings: *-st* = du, *-t* = er/sie/es |
| **Schreiben** | You MUST use correct endings in your messages |
| **Sprechen** | Wrong endings sound very noticeable — practice! |

## Key Tips

1. **Learn the ending rhythm:** -e, -st, -t, -en, -t, -en
2. **Buffer -e-:** arbeiten → du arbeit**e**st (NOT arbeitst)
3. **heißen trap:** du heiß**t** (NOT heißst) — ß already has s
4. **wir/sie/Sie = -en** — same as infinitive! Easy points.
5. **Position 2:** Verb is ALWAYS in position 2 in German statements

## Speed Drill

| Fill in | Answer |
|---|---|
| Ich ___ in Berlin. (wohnen) | **wohne** |
| Wo ___ du? (arbeiten) | **arbeitest** |
| Er ___ Deutsch. (lernen) | **lernt** |
| Wie ___ Sie? (heißen) | **heißen** |

> 🇻🇳 *Mẹo: Trong bài viết/nói, sai đuôi động từ = mất điểm rõ nhất. Luyện đuôi -e/-st/-t cho đến khi phản xạ!*
