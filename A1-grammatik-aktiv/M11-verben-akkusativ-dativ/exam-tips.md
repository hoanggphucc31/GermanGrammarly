# Module 11 — Exam Tips: Verben mit Akkusativ und Dativ

> 🎯 **Goethe A1:** Verbs and their cases are essential for understanding "who does what to whom" in Lesen and Hören, and for writing correct sentences in Schreiben.

---

## How This Grammar Appears

| Section | What to Watch |
|---|---|
| **Lesen** | Identifying the receiver (Dativ) vs the object (Akkusativ) in emails (e.g., "Ich schicke dir das Foto"). |
| **Schreiben** | Writing simple requests or stating actions correctly: "Können Sie mir helfen?", "Ich bringe den Kuchen." |
| **Hören** | Listening for `ihm`, `ihr`, `mir`, `dir` to know who receives something. |
| **Sprechen** | Asking for help: "Können Sie mir helfen?" or thanking: "Ich danke Ihnen." |

## Key Tips

1. **Memorize the Dativ-Only Verbs!**
   * *helfen, danken, gehören, gefallen, antworten, schmecken*. 
   * If you see these, automatically look for the Dativ form (mir, dir, dem, der, etc.).

2. **Word Order for Gifts and Giving**
   * If writing an email about a birthday or party (very common in A1 Schreiben), remember the order: **Person (Dat) before Thing (Akk)**.
   * *Ich schenke **meinem Freund** (Dat) **ein Buch** (Akk).*

3. **`ihm` vs `ihn`**
   * `ihm` (with M) = Dativ (him/it, receiver) → *Ich helfe ihm.*
   * `ihn` (with N) = Akkusativ (him, direct object) → *Ich sehe ihn.*

## Speed Drill

| Situation | Correct Sentence |
|---|---|
| You want to thank your teacher (female). | Ich danke **der Lehrerin**. (or: Ich danke **Ihnen**.) |
| You want to give the book to the man. | Ich gebe **dem Mann** das Buch. |
| You need help. | Können Sie **mir** helfen? |

> 🇻🇳 *Thuộc lòng nhóm động từ Dativ-only (helfen, danken...) vì tiếng Việt hay nhầm sang tân ngữ trực tiếp. Khi viết câu có 2 tân ngữ, luôn ưu tiên Người (Dat) đứng trước Vật (Akk).*
