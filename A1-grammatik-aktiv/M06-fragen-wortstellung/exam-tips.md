# Module 06 — Exam Tips: Fragen & Wortstellung

> 🎯 **Goethe A1:** Questions appear in ALL sections — Sprechen, Hören, Lesen, Schreiben.

---

## How This Grammar Appears

| Section | What to Watch |
|---|---|
| **Sprechen Teil 2** | You must ASK questions to your partner using W-words |
| **Sprechen Teil 3** | You must make REQUESTS (yes/no pattern) |
| **Hören** | Recognize whether a question is W- or yes/no to anticipate the answer type |
| **Lesen** | Form questions use W-words (Wie heißen Sie? Wo wohnen Sie?) |
| **Schreiben** | Write messages with correct word order and questions |

## Key Tips

1. **wo ≠ woher ≠ wohin** — the #1 mistake in Sprechen
2. **V2 always** — verb in position 2 in statements, even when time comes first
3. **Doch!** — use it when someone asks a negative question and you disagree
4. **Yes/No = verb first** — don't forget to invert!
5. **Sprechen Teil 2:** Prepare 3-4 W-questions about your partner

## Speed Drill

| Fill in | Answer |
|---|---|
| ___ wohnst du? | **Wo** |
| ___ kommst du? | **Woher** |
| Am Montag ___ ich. (arbeiten) | **arbeite** (V2!) |
| ___ du Deutsch? (yes/no) | **Sprichst** |

> 🇻🇳 *Phần thi Sprechen: chuẩn bị sẵn 3-4 câu W-Frage. Nhớ wo/woher/wohin. Doch! dùng khi phản bác câu hỏi phủ định.*
