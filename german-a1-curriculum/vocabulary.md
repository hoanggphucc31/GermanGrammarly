## How to Use This List

1. **Read aloud** each row to internalise pronunciation.
2. **Cover** either the German or English column and try to recall the missing side.
3. **Practice plurals** by covering the plural column and forming the plural from the singular.
4. **Group study**: focus on one topic per day.
5. **Make flashcards** (physical or digital) using the three columns.

Feel free to extend this list with additional words you encounter in your lessons.
