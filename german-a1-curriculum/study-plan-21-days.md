# 21-Day Daily Study Plan

## Week 1: Foundations

### Day 1

- Learn `der, die, das`
- Memorize 30 nouns with articles
- Read Topic 1 Lesson 1.1
- Do Exercises 1-15 from Topic 1

### Day 2

- Study plural forms
- Make your own list of 20 singular/plural pairs
- Finish Topic 1 exercises
- Review answers

### Day 3

- Study personal pronouns
- Make 20 short sentences with `ich, du, er, sie`
- Read Topic 2 Lesson 2.1

### Day 4

- Study `sein` and `haben`
- Conjugate them 10 times each
- Finish Topic 2 exercises 1-19

### Day 5

- Study possessive articles
- Write sentences with `mein, dein, sein, ihr`
- Finish Topic 2 exercises 20-30

### Day 6

- Review Topics 1 and 2
- Re-do wrong exercises
- Speak aloud 30 example sentences

### Day 7

- Mini test on Topics 1 and 2
- Write a self-introduction in German

## Week 2: Verbs and Sentence Structure

### Day 8

- Study regular present tense verbs
- Conjugate 10 verbs
- Read Topic 3 Lesson 3.1

### Day 9

- Study irregular vowel-change verbs
- Practice `fahren, lesen, sprechen`
- Do Topic 3 exercises 1-10

### Day 10

- Study separable verbs
- Make 15 sentences with `aufstehen, anrufen, einkaufen`
- Do Topic 3 exercises 11-30

### Day 11

- Study basic word order
- Practice 20 normal sentences
- Read Topic 4 Lesson 4.1

### Day 12

- Study questions
- Write 10 yes/no questions and 10 W-questions
- Do Topic 4 exercises 1-10

### Day 13

- Study `nicht` and `kein`
- Write 20 negative sentences
- Finish Topic 4 exercises 11-30

### Day 14

- Review Topics 3 and 4
- Speak and write short dialogue practice

## Week 3: Cases, Prepositions, and Communication

### Day 15

- Study nominative vs accusative
- Focus on `der -> den`, `ein -> einen`
- Read Topic 5 Lesson 5.1

### Day 16

- Practice accusative objects in 20 sentences
- Do Topic 5 exercises 1-10

### Day 17

- Study dative basics and prepositions
- Memorize `mit, nach, aus, bei, von, zu`
- Finish Topic 5 exercises 11-30

### Day 18

- Study modal verbs
- Write 15 sentences with `konnen, mussen, wollen, mochten`
- Read Topic 6 Lesson 6.1

### Day 19

- Study imperative and time expressions
- Practice `um, am, im`
- Finish Topic 6 exercises

### Day 20

- Review all 6 topics
- Re-do difficult exercises
- Make a one-page grammar summary

### Day 21

- Take the full 50-question practice test
- Check answers
- Review weak grammar points
