## Writing and Speaking

For writing and speaking tasks, use these simple checks:

- Is the answer clear?
- Is the grammar A1 level?
- Are the sentences short?
- Is the task complete?

Good writing model:

`Hallo Anna, ich bin heute krank. Ich komme nicht zum Kurs. Bis morgen. Viele Gruesse, Sara`
