# A1 Certificate Study Guide

## Goal

This guide helps you prepare for a basic European A1 German certificate exam.

## Main Exam Areas

1. Lesen
You read short texts, signs, messages, emails, or notices.

2. Hoeren
You understand very short daily-life information.

3. Schreiben
You write short personal messages and simple forms.

4. Sprechen
You introduce yourself, ask simple questions, and answer short questions.

## A1 Topics You Must Know

- name, age, country, city
- family
- food and drinks
- home and furniture
- school and work
- days, time, numbers
- shopping
- transport
- hobbies
- simple directions and places

## Exam Tips

1. Read the task first.
2. Look for key words.
3. Keep your German short and clear.
4. Do not try to use difficult grammar.
5. In writing, use simple sentence patterns.
6. In speaking, speak slowly and clearly.

## Useful A1 Sentence Frames

- `Ich heisse ...`
- `Ich komme aus ...`
- `Ich wohne in ...`
- `Ich bin ... Jahre alt.`
- `Ich habe ...`
- `Ich moechte ...`
- `Ich kann ...`
- `Wann ...?`
- `Wo ...?`
- `Wie viel ...?`

## Self-Check

This guide is strictly A1 level.
