# 📋 A1 Certificate Study Guide

> Understanding the **exam structure** = winning half the battle! This guide shows you exactly what the A1 exam tests and how to tackle each section effectively.

---

## 1 · A1 Exam Structure (Goethe-Zertifikat)

| Section | German Name | Time | Questions | Content |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Lesen** (Reading) | 25 min | ~15 items | Read signs, emails, messages, notices |
| 2 | **Hören** (Listening) | 20 min | ~15 items | Listen to announcements, short conversations |
| 3 | **Schreiben** (Writing) | 20 min | 2 tasks | Fill in a form + write a short email/message |
| 4 | **Sprechen** (Speaking) | 15 min | 3 parts | Self-introduction, Q&A with cards, polite requests |

> Total: approximately **60-80 minutes**. You need **≥ 60%** to pass.

## 3 · Strategies for Each Section

### 📖 Lesen (Reading)

| Tip | Details |
|---|---|
| ✅ Read the **questions first**, then read the text | Helps you focus on the information you need |
| ✅ Look for **keywords** | You don't need to understand 100% — just find the right info |
| ✅ Watch out for **nicht, kein, nur** | Negative words often reverse the meaning |
| ❌ Don't translate word by word | Too slow and unnecessary |

### 🎧 Hören (Listening)

| Tip | Details |
|---|---|
| ✅ Read questions **before listening** | Know what to listen for |
| ✅ Listen for **numbers, days, times, names** first | These are the most commonly tested |
| ✅ Listen **twice** — 1st time for gist, 2nd to confirm | |
| ❌ Don't panic if you don't understand everything | Just catch the keywords |

### ✍️ Schreiben (Writing)

| Tip | Details |
|---|---|
| ✅ Write **short and correct** sentences | No need for complex structures |
| ✅ Use **familiar sentence patterns** | Ich heiße..., Ich wohne in..., Ich möchte... |
| ✅ Answer **all 3 points** in the prompt | Missing 1 point = lost marks |
| ✅ Use proper email format | Liebe/Lieber..., Viele Grüße, [name] |
| ❌ Don't write too long | 30-40 words is enough |

### 🗣️ Sprechen (Speaking)

| Tip | Details |
|---|---|
| ✅ Speak **slowly and clearly** | Better than speaking fast with errors |
| ✅ Prepare your **self-introduction** | Name, Alter, Land, Stadt, Beruf, Familie, Hobby |
| ✅ Know how to **ask + answer** about cards | Wie viel kostet...? Wann...? Wo...? |
| ✅ Use **bitte, danke, Entschuldigung** | Makes a polite impression |
| ❌ Don't stay silent too long | Say *"Moment, bitte"* if you need to think |

## 4 · "Lifesaver" Sentence Templates for the Exam

### Self-Introduction (Sprechen Teil 1)

```
Hallo! Mein Name ist [name].
Ich bin [age] Jahre alt.
Ich komme aus [country].
Ich wohne in [city].
Ich bin [profession] / Ich studiere [subject].
Ich spreche [language] und ein bisschen Deutsch.
Meine Hobbys sind [hobby] und [hobby].
```

### Asking for Information (Sprechen Teil 2)

```
Wie heißen Sie?
Woher kommen Sie?
Wo wohnen Sie?
Was machen Sie beruflich?
Haben Sie Kinder?
Was ist Ihr Hobby?
Wie viel kostet das?
Wann beginnt der Kurs?
```

### Polite Requests (Sprechen Teil 3)

```
Können Sie das bitte wiederholen?
Können Sie bitte langsam sprechen?
Ich möchte einen Kaffee, bitte.
Entschuldigung, wo ist die Toilette?
Darf ich bitte...?
```

### Writing an Email (Schreiben Teil 2)

```
Liebe/Lieber [name],

vielen Dank für deine E-Mail.
Ich [answer question 1].
[Answer question 2].
[Answer question 3].

Viele Grüße
[Your name]
```

## 5 · Pre-Exam Checklist

- [ ] Know conjugation tables: sein, haben, 6 modal verbs
- [ ] Know article tables: Nom. / Akk. / Dat.
- [ ] Know 12 W-question words
- [ ] Know the difference between nicht / kein
- [ ] Know um / am / im
- [ ] Have your self-introduction memorized
- [ ] Know how to write a short email (30-40 words)
- [ ] Completed both Mock Tests
- [ ] Get a good night's sleep before the exam 😴
