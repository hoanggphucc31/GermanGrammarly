## Part 2: Short Message Tasks

### Task 1

Write a short message to your teacher.

Include:

- greeting
- you are sick today
- you cannot come to class
- closing

Write 3-5 short sentences.

### Task 2

Write a short message to a friend.

Include:

- day
- time
- place
- what you want to do

Write 3-5 short sentences.

### Task 3

Write a short self-introduction.

Include:

- name
- country
- city
- age
- language

Write 4-6 short sentences.

## Useful Exam Phrases

- `Hallo ...`
- `Ich bin heute krank.`
- `Ich komme heute nicht.`
- `Wir treffen uns am ...`
- `Um ... Uhr`
- `Bis bald`
- `Viele Gruesse`
