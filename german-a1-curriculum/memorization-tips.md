# Tips to Memorize German Grammar Effectively

1. Learn every noun with its article.
   Example: not `Tisch`, but `der Tisch`.

2. Use color coding.
   Example:
   - blue for `der`
   - red for `die`
   - green for `das`

3. Memorize formulas, not only rules.
   Example:
   - `Subject + verb + ...`
   - `Question word + verb + subject + ...`
   - `Subject + modal + ... + infinitive`

4. Make very short daily sentences.
   Example:
   - `Ich bin mude.`
   - `Ich habe Zeit.`
   - `Ich kann kommen.`

5. Practice aloud.
   German grammar becomes easier when your mouth remembers the pattern.

6. Compare similar forms.
   Example:
   - `der Mann` vs `ich sehe den Mann`
   - `das ist kein Auto` vs `das Auto ist nicht neu`

7. Review mistakes in a notebook.
   Make a personal list called: `My German mistakes`.

8. Repeat after 1 day, 3 days, and 7 days.

9. Write mini-dialogues.

10. Focus on high-frequency structures first.
    Especially:
    - `sein`
    - `haben`
    - present tense
    - questions
    - `nicht/kein`
    - accusative masculine
    - modal verbs
