# Lesson 9 Workbook

## Focus

Nominative and accusative in simple sentences.

## Learning Targets

- identify subject and direct object
- use `den` and `einen`
- build short accusative sentences

## Exercise Order

1. Vocabulary Warm-Up
Questions `1-10` in `../exercises-08-15.md`

2. Case Form Practice
Questions `11-25`

3. Error Correction and Sentence Building
Questions `26-40`

4. Translation and Meaning Check
Questions `41-60`

5. Review Check
Questions `61-70`

## Suggested Timing

- Warm-up: 10 minutes
- Form practice: 15 minutes
- Correction: 15 minutes
- Translation: 15 minutes
- Review: 15 minutes
