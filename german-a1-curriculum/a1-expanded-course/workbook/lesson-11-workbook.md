# Lesson 11 Workbook

## Focus

Accusative pronouns and negative accusative forms.

## Learning Targets

- use `mich`, `dich`, `ihn`, `uns`, `euch`
- use `keinen` correctly
- control accusative object forms in short sentences

## Exercise Order

1. Vocabulary Warm-Up
Questions `1-10` in `../exercises-08-15.md`

2. Pronoun and Article Practice
Questions `11-25`

3. Error Correction and Sentence Building
Questions `26-40`

4. Translation and Guided Production
Questions `41-55`

5. Review Check
Questions `56-70`

## Suggested Timing

- Warm-up: 10 minutes
- Form practice: 15 minutes
- Correction: 15 minutes
- Production: 15 minutes
- Review: 15 minutes
