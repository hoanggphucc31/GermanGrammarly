# Lesson 0 Workbook

## Focus

Self-introduction, basic personal information, and simple reading comprehension.

## Learning Targets

- say your name
- say where you are from
- say where you live
- say your age
- say your language and job

## Exercise Order

1. Vocabulary Warm-Up
Questions `1-10` in `../exercises-00-07.md`

2. Basic Identity Sentences
Questions `11-22`

3. Question and Answer Practice
Questions `23-35`

4. Dialogue and Reading Practice
Questions `41-50`

5. Personal Production and Review
Questions `51-70`

## Suggested Timing

- Warm-up: 10 minutes
- Sentence practice: 15 minutes
- Question practice: 15 minutes
- Reading and dialogue: 15 minutes
- Personal writing and review: 15 minutes
