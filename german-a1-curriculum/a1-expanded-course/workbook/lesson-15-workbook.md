# Lesson 15 Workbook

## Focus

Imperative, time expressions, and basic connectors.

## Learning Targets

- form simple commands
- use `um`, `am`, and `im`
- use `und`, `aber`, and `oder` in short sentences

## Exercise Order

1. Vocabulary Warm-Up
Questions `1-10` in `../exercises-08-15.md`

2. Command and Time Practice
Questions `11-25`

3. Error Correction and Sentence Building
Questions `26-40`

4. Translation and Guided Use
Questions `41-55`

5. Review Check
Questions `56-70`

## Suggested Timing

- Warm-up: 10 minutes
- Form practice: 15 minutes
- Correction: 15 minutes
- Guided use: 15 minutes
- Review: 15 minutes
