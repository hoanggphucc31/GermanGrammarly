# Lesson 3 Workbook

## Focus

Personal pronouns in simple sentences.

## Learning Targets

- choose the correct subject pronoun
- replace nouns with pronouns
- match pronouns with correct verb forms

## Exercise Order

1. Vocabulary Warm-Up
Questions `1-10` in `../exercises-00-07.md`

2. Pronoun Replacement
Questions `11-25`

3. Correction and Sentence Building
Questions `26-40`

4. Translation and Ordering Practice
Questions `41-60`

5. Free Sentence Review
Questions `61-70`

## Suggested Timing

- Warm-up: 10 minutes
- Replacement: 15 minutes
- Correction: 15 minutes
- Translation: 15 minutes
- Review: 15 minutes
