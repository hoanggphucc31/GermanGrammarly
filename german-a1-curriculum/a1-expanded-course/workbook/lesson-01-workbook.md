# Lesson 1 Workbook

## Focus

Articles, gender, and article-noun combinations.

## Learning Targets

- recognize `der`, `die`, `das`
- use `ein` and `eine`
- build short noun phrases correctly

## Exercise Order

1. Vocabulary Warm-Up
Questions `1-10` in `../exercises-00-07.md`

2. Article Recognition
Questions `11-25`

3. Error Correction and Sentence Building
Questions `26-40`

4. Practical Word Use
Questions `41-55`

5. Review Check
Questions `56-70`

## Suggested Timing

- Warm-up: 10 minutes
- Recognition: 15 minutes
- Correction: 15 minutes
- Production: 15 minutes
- Review: 15 minutes
