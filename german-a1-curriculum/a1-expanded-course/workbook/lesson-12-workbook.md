# Lesson 12 Workbook

## Focus

Dative pronouns and common dative sentence patterns.

## Learning Targets

- use `mir`, `dir`, `ihm`, `ihr`, `uns`, `euch`, `Ihnen`
- use `helfen`, `danken`, `gefallen`, `gehoeren`
- write short dative sentences naturally

## Exercise Order

1. Vocabulary Warm-Up
Questions `1-10` in `../exercises-08-15.md`

2. Pronoun and Dative Form Practice
Questions `11-25`

3. Error Correction and Sentence Building
Questions `26-40`

4. Translation and Guided Use
Questions `41-55`

5. Review Check
Questions `56-70`

## Suggested Timing

- Warm-up: 10 minutes
- Form practice: 15 minutes
- Correction: 15 minutes
- Guided use: 15 minutes
- Review: 15 minutes
