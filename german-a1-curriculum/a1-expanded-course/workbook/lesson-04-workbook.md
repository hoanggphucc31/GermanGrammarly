# Lesson 4 Workbook

## Focus

`sein`, `haben`, and basic possessive articles.

## Learning Targets

- conjugate `sein` and `haben`
- use simple possessive articles
- write short identity and possession sentences

1. Vocabulary Warm-Up
Questions `1-10` in `../exercises-00-07.md`

2. Verb Form Practice
Questions `11-25`

3. Error Correction and Sentence Building
Questions `26-40`

4. Possessive and Translation Practice
Questions `41-60`

5. Review Check
Questions `61-70`

## Suggested Timing

- Warm-up: 10 minutes
- Verb forms: 15 minutes
- Correction: 15 minutes
- Possessives: 15 minutes
- Review: 15 minutes
