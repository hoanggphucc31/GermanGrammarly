# Lesson 5 Workbook

## Focus

Present tense verb conjugation.

## Learning Targets

- conjugate regular verbs
- use common irregular forms
- build short present-tense sentences

## Exercise Order

1. Vocabulary Warm-Up
Questions `1-10` in `../exercises-00-07.md`

2. Core Verb Conjugation
Questions `11-25`

3. Error Correction and Sentence Building
Questions `26-44`

4. Translation and Ordering Practice
Questions `45-55`

5. Review Check
Questions `56-70`

## Suggested Timing

- Warm-up: 10 minutes
- Conjugation: 15 minutes
- Sentence control: 20 minutes
- Translation: 10 minutes
- Review: 15 minutes
