# Topic 1 Answer Key

1. `Der` Mann ist Lehrer.  
Why: `Mann` is masculine.

2. `Die` Frau heisst Anna.  
Why: `Frau` is feminine.

3. `Das` Kind ist mude.  
Why: `Kind` is neuter.

Why: `Auto` is neuter.

Why: `Lampe` is feminine.
