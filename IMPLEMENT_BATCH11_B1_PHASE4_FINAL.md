## 2. Pedagogical Highlights (Expert Additions)

As with previous batches, we maintained a high standard of pedagogical clarity by supplementing the core textbook material with "Expert Additions" designed specifically for self-learners and Goethe exam candidates.

### M11: Das Verb "lassen"
- **The "lassen" vs. "gelassen" Trap:** Explicitly documented the rule for Perfekt tense (using the double infinitive "lassen" when there's another verb vs. using "gelassen" when it's the main verb).
- **The 3 Functions Diagnostic:** Added a clear table contrasting Erlauben (Allow), Veranlassen (Service), and Nicht tun/Aufhören (Stop/Leave state) to help students parse the meaning from context.

### M12: Positionsverben & Genitiv
- **The Golden Rule:** Formalized the "Wo? (State) + Dativ" vs "Wohin? (Action) + Akkusativ" logic for the 4 key verb pairs (sitzen/setzen, liegen/legen, etc.).
- **The Complete Case Map:** Added a master table showing Nominativ, Akkusativ, Dativ, and the newly learned Genitiv on a single page for comprehensive review.
- **📚 Preposition Focus (Expert Addition):** Introduced the 4 essential B1 Genitiv prepositions (wegen, trotz, während, statt) as a pedagogical supplement beyond the core scope of Kap. 63.

### M13: N-Deklination & Adjektive als Nomen
- **N-Deklination Groupings:** Organized the seemingly random N-Deklination nouns into logical groups (Male people/animals, Nationalities, Academic titles ending in -ent/-ist).
- **Capitalization Rule:** Clarified the rule for abstract concepts (etwas/nichts + Adjektiv mit -es).
- **Comparison Table:** Created a specific "Expert Addition" table comparing N-Deklination vs. Adjektive als Nomen, as these are the two biggest sources of "-en" ending confusion for B1 students.
