# Module 12 — Flashcards: Positionsverben & Genitiv

## Verb Pairs (State vs Action)
| Front | Back |
|---|---|
| State (Wo?): "The book lies on the table." | Das Buch **liegt** auf dem Tisch. |
| Action (Wohin?): "I lay the book on the table." | Ich **lege** das Buch auf den Tisch. |
| State (Wo?): "The bottle stands there." | Die Flasche **steht** da. |
| Action (Wohin?): "I stand/put the bottle there." | Ich **stelle** die Flasche dahin. |

## Perfekt Forms
| Front | Back |
|---|---|
| Perfekt of "sitzen" (to be sitting) | hat ge**sessen** |
| Perfekt of "setzen" (to seat) | hat ge**setzt** |
| Perfekt of "liegen" (to be lying) | hat ge**legen** |
| Perfekt of "legen" (to lay) | hat ge**legt** |

## Genitiv
| Front | Back |
|---|---|
| Translate: "The father's car." | Das Auto **des Vaters**. |
| Prepositions requiring Genitiv (Name 4) | **wegen**, **trotz**, **während**, **statt** |
| Translate: "Because of the rain..." | **Wegen des Regens**... |
| Translate: "During the vacation..." | **Während des Urlaubs**... |

## Case Rules
| Front | Back |
|---|---|
| "Wo?" (Where?) requires which case? | **Dativ** |
| "Wohin?" (Where to?) requires which case? | **Akkusativ** |
| Which genders get an `-s` or `-es` ending in Genitiv? | **Maskulin** and **Neutrum** |
