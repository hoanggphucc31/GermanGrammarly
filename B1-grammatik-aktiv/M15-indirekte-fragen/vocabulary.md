# Vocabulary: Indirekte Fragen

## Introductory Phrases (Einleitungssätze)

| German | English | 🇻🇳 Vietnamese |
|---|---|---|
| **Wissen Sie,...?** | Do you know...? | Bạn có biết...? |
| **Können Sie mir sagen,...?** | Can you tell me...? | Bạn có thể nói cho tôi biết...? |
| **Darf ich fragen,...?** | May I ask...? | Tôi có thể hỏi...? |
| **Ich möchte gern wissen,...** | I would like to know... | Tôi muốn biết... |
| **Ich wüsste sehr gern,...** | I would really like to know... | Tôi rất muốn biết... (rất lịch sự) |
| **Ich bin nicht sicher,...** | I am not sure... | Tôi không chắc... |
| **Ich habe keine Ahnung,...** | I have no idea... | Tôi không có ý tưởng gì / Không biết... |
| **Könnten Sie mir sagen,...?** | Could you tell me...? | Bạn có thể vui lòng nói cho tôi... (rất lịch sự) |

## Important W-Words (Review)

| German | English |
|---|---|
| **wer** | who |
| **wen / wem** | who (Akk) / who (Dat) |
| **was** | what |
| **wo / wohin / woher** | where / where to / where from |
| **wann** | when |
| **wie lange** | how long |
| **wie oft** | how often |
| **warum / wieso / weshalb** | why |
| **wie** | how |

## Useful Nouns & Verbs for Information Queries

| German | English | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **die Auskunft** | information | Können Sie mir eine Auskunft geben? |
| **sich erkundigen (nach)** | to inquire (about) | Ich möchte mich danach erkundigen, ob... |
| **Bescheid sagen/geben** | to let someone know | Sagen Sie mir Bescheid, ob Sie kommen. |
| **stattfinden** | to take place | Weißt du, wann das Konzert stattfindet? |
