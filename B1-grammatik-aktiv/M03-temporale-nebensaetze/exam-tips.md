# Module 03 — Exam Tips: Temporale Nebensätze

> 🎯 **Goethe B1:** als/wenn is tested in **Sprechen** (telling stories) and **Schreiben** (describing experiences). Getting this right shows B1-level grammar competence.

---

## How This Grammar Appears

| Section | What to Watch |
|---|---|
| **Sprechen** | Part 2: "Erzählen Sie von einem Erlebnis." → Use als for the story. |
| **Schreiben** | Describing experiences: "**Als** ich nach Deutschland kam, ..." |
| **Lesen** | Biographical texts with als-clauses. |
| **Hören** | Interviews about life stories. |

## Key Tips

1. **Storytelling Formula (Sprechen)**
   * Start your story with `als`:
   * *"**Als** ich 18 **war**, bin ich zum ersten Mal nach Deutschland geflogen."*
   * Then continue with normal past tense.

2. **The "immer wenn" Safety Net**
   * If it's repeated past and you're not sure, add "immer" before wenn:
   * *"**Immer wenn** ich Heimweh **hatte**, habe ich meine Eltern angerufen."*
   * The word "immer" makes it 100% clear it's repeated.

3. **ob vs wenn — The Test**
   * Replace with "whether" → if it works → **ob**
   * *Ich weiß nicht, **ob** (= whether) er kommt.* ✅
   * Replace with "if/when (condition)" → **wenn**
   * ***Wenn** (= if/when) du kommst, ruf mich an.* ✅

4. **Word Order Reminder**
   * als/wenn clause: verb → **END**
   * When als/wenn clause comes first: main clause verb → **position 2** (inversion!)

> 🇻🇳 *Mẹo: Trong bài Sprechen, khi kể chuyện, hãy bắt đầu bằng "Als ich... war/kam/wurde, ..." — đây là cách mở đầu chuẩn B1. Sau đó chuyển sang kể bình thường bằng Perfekt.*
