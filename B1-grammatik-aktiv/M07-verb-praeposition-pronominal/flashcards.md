# Module 07 — Flashcards: Verb + Präposition & Pronominaladverbien

## Fixed Prepositions
| Front | Back |
|---|---|
| warten ...? (wait for) | warten **auf** (Akkusativ) |
| sich interessieren ...? (interested in) | sich interessieren **für** (Akkusativ) |
| denken ...? (think of/about) | denken **an** (Akkusativ) |
| träumen ...? (dream of) | träumen **von** (Dativ) |
| fragen ...? (ask about/for) | fragen **nach** (Dativ) |

## Person vs. Thing
| Front | Back |
|---|---|
| "What are you waiting for?" (Thing) | **Worauf** wartest du? |
| "Who are you waiting for?" (Person) | **Auf wen** wartest du? |
| "I am dreaming of IT." (Thing) | Ich träume **davon**. |
| "I am dreaming of HIM." (Person) | Ich träume **von ihm**. |

## Tricky Pairs
| Front | Back |
|---|---|
| sich freuen **auf** vs. sich freuen **über** | **auf** = looking forward to (Future). **über** = happy about (Present/Past gift or event). |
| sprechen **mit** vs. sprechen **über** | **mit** = speaking WITH someone (Dat). **über** = speaking ABOUT something/someone (Akk). |
