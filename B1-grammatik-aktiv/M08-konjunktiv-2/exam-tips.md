# Module 08 — Exam Tips: Konjunktiv II

## Goethe Zertifikat B1 Strategy

### 1. Sprechen (Speaking Part 1: Plan something together)

* **The Ultimate Politeness Hack:** You MUST use Konjunktiv II to make suggestions and react to your partner. If you only use *wollen* or *können*, you sound like an A1 student and will lose points for register/politeness.
    * ❌ A1/A2: *Ich will am Freitag treffen.*
    * ✅ B1: *Ich **würde** mich gern am Freitag treffen. **Hättest** du da Zeit?*
* **Giving Advice (Part 2: Presentation):** When giving advice or talking about alternatives in your presentation, use Konjunktiv II.
    * *An deiner Stelle **würde** ich...*
    * *Man **sollte** öfter Sport machen.*

### 2. Schreiben (Writing Part 1 & 2)

* **Writing to a friend (Part 1):** Use it to express a wish or regret.
    * *Es war so eine tolle Party! Schade, dass du nicht kommen konntest. Ich wünschte, du **wärest** auch da **gewesen**!* (I wish you had been there too! - Konjunktiv II Vergangenheit).
* **Writing a formal opinion (Part 2):** Use it for giving advice or suggesting a better scenario.
    * *Meiner Meinung nach **sollte** die Stadt mehr tun.* (In my opinion, the city should do more.)
    * *Es **wäre** viel besser, wenn wir weniger Plastik benutzen **würden**.* (It would be much better if we used less plastic.)

### 3. Lesen (Reading) & Hören (Listening)

* **Spotting unreal conditions:** In reading texts, pay attention to sentences with *wenn*. Are they facts (Präsens) or hypotheticals (Konjunktiv II)? This changes the meaning of the whole paragraph.
    * Example: *Wenn er mehr Geld **hätte**, **würde** er expandieren.* (Fact: He does NOT have money, so he is NOT expanding.) If the reading comprehension question asks "Is he expanding his business?", the answer is "No".

### 4. The 80/20 Rule for the Exam
Don't try to memorize the "Real" Konjunktiv II forms of strong verbs (like *käme*, *ginge*, *äße*). They sound old-fashioned and are almost never used in modern spoken B1 German. 
**Only memorize:**
* wäre, hätte, würde
* könnte, müsste, sollte, dürfte, wollte
For everything else, just use **würde + Infinitiv**!
