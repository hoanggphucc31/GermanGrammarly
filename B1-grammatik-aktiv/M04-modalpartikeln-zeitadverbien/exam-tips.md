# Module 04 — Exam Tips: Modalpartikeln & Zeitadverbien

> 🎯 **Goethe B1:** Modalpartikeln appear mainly in **Sprechen** (natural conversation). Zeitadverbien (schon/noch/erst) appear in all sections. Using particles makes you sound B1-level instead of A2!

---

## How This Grammar Appears

| Section | What to Watch |
|---|---|
| **Sprechen** | Use denn/mal/doch in your conversation to sound natural. |
| **Schreiben** | Use schon/noch/nicht mehr in descriptions. Avoid Modalpartikeln in formal letters! |
| **Hören** | Recognizing particles helps you understand the speaker's attitude. |
| **Lesen** | schon mal/noch nie appear in survey/questionnaire texts. |

## Key Tips

1. **Sprechen: The "Natural German" Toolkit**
   * Add **denn** to every question: *"Wie lange lernst du **denn** Deutsch?"*
   * Add **mal** to every request: *"Können Sie **mal** wiederholen?"*
   * These two particles alone make you sound 50% more natural!

2. **Schreiben: Formal vs Informal**
   * ❌ Don't use Modalpartikeln in formal Schreiben (Beschwerde, Anfrage).
   * ✅ Do use them in informal emails to friends.
   * ✅ Always use schon/noch/erst/nicht mehr in any writing.

3. **schon mal for Experience Questions**
   * Very common in Sprechen Part 1:
   * *"Waren Sie **schon mal** in Deutschland?"*
   * *"Haben Sie **schon mal** eine Prüfung gemacht?"*
   * Answer: *"Ja, ich war **schon mal**..."* or *"Nein, **noch nie**."*

4. **nicht mehr for Life Changes**
   * Perfect for describing changes:
   * *"Ich wohne **nicht mehr** in meiner Heimatstadt."*
   * *"Ich arbeite **nicht mehr** bei BMW."*

> 🇻🇳 *Mẹo: Trong bài Sprechen, hãy thêm "denn" vào câu hỏi và "mal" vào yêu cầu. Chỉ 2 từ nhỏ này sẽ khiến giám khảo đánh giá bạn tự nhiên hơn rất nhiều. Nhưng đừng dùng chúng trong thư formal!*
