# Module 09 — Exam Tips: Passiv

## Goethe Zertifikat B1 Strategy

### 1. Lesen (Reading)

* **The Ultimate Passive Test:** The reading section (especially Teil 2: Newspaper Articles and Teil 5: Rules/Regulations) is heavily reliant on the passive voice.
* **Identify the true subject:** In a passive sentence, the grammatical subject receives the action.
    * *Den Kindern wird geholfen.* (The children are being helped. - Notice that "Den Kindern" is Dativ! The verb "helfen" requires Dativ, even in a passive sentence where it acts as the logical subject).
* **Do not ignore `wurde`:** If you see `wurde`, it means the event happened in the **past**. This is often the key to answering true/false questions about timelines.

### 2. Schreiben (Writing Part 2 & 3)

* **Writing a formal opinion (Part 2):** Using one or two passive sentences makes your text look very professional and objective.
    * *Es muss mehr für die Umwelt **getan werden**.* (More must be done for the environment. - Passive with a modal verb!)
    * *Das Problem kann nicht sofort **gelöst werden**.* (The problem cannot be solved immediately.)
* **Writing a formal email (Part 3):**
    * *Mir **wurde gesagt**, dass der Termin ausfällt.* (I was told that the appointment is cancelled.)

### 3. Hören (Listening)

* **Listen for the Partizip II at the end:** When listening to news reports (Teil 1 or Teil 3), if you hear "wird" early in the sentence, wait for the end! If the last word has a "ge-" (or ends in -ieren/-t), it's a passive sentence.
    * *Das Wetter **wird** morgen wieder besser.* (Active - The weather will become better).
    * *Das Wetter **wird** von Experten genau **beobachtet**.* (Passive - The weather is being observed closely by experts).

### 4. Sprechen (Speaking)

* **Use it sparingly:** In daily conversation (and thus in the speaking exam), the active voice is preferred. Don't force passive sentences where an active one sounds more natural.
    * Good: *Man baut hier ein neues Haus.* (Active with "man")
    * Stiff: *Hier wird ein neues Haus gebaut.* (Passive - totally fine, but less conversational).
* **Exception:** If you don't know who did something, use the passive!
    * *Mein Handy **ist gestohlen worden**!* (My phone has been stolen!)

### 5. Passiv mit Modalverb — The B1 Exam Favorite

This pattern is extremely common in B1 exam texts. Recognize and produce it!

* **In Lesen (Reading):** Rules and regulations almost always use this form.
    * *Das Formular **muss** bis Freitag **ausgefüllt werden**.* (The form must be filled out by Friday.)
    * *Handys **dürfen** nicht **benutzt werden**.* (Cell phones must not be used.)
* **In Schreiben (Writing):** Use it to sound formal and objective.
    * *Meiner Meinung nach **sollte** mehr Geld für Bildung **investiert werden**.* (In my opinion, more money should be invested in education.)
* **Active → Passive Conversion Trick:** If you see *Man muss/kann/soll ...*, you can always convert it to a passive sentence.
    * Active: *Man **muss** die Regeln **beachten**.* (One must follow the rules.)
    * Passive: *Die Regeln **müssen beachtet werden**.* (The rules must be followed.)
