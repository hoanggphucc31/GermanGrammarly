# Module 11: Das Verb "lassen"

> **CEFR Goal:** Can use the verb "lassen" correctly in its three main communicative functions: allowing something, having something done by someone else (services), and stopping an action.
>
> **Scope:** 📘 Book Core: Das Verb "lassen" (Kapitel 61). 📚 Expert Addition: Diagnostic table for the 3 functions and the Perfekt tense trap (lassen vs. gelassen).
>
> 🇻🇳 *Module này tập trung vào động từ "lassen" — một trong những động từ đa năng và dùng nhiều nhất trong tiếng Đức. Bạn sẽ học cách dùng nó để nói về việc sử dụng dịch vụ (như đi cắt tóc, sửa xe), cho phép ai làm gì, hoặc bảo ai đó dừng lại.*

---

## Part 1: The Three Functions of "lassen"

The verb *lassen* is a shapeshifter. Depending on the context, it means completely different things.

### Function 1: Erlauben (To allow / let)
Used when someone gives permission to someone else.
* *Die Mutter **lässt** die Kinder draußen **spielen**.* 
  (The mother lets/allows the children play outside.)
* *Lass mich in Ruhe!* (Leave me alone! -> literal: Let me in peace!)

### Function 2: Veranlassen (To have something done / get someone to do it)
Used when you use a service or order someone to do a task for you. You don't do it yourself!
* *Ich **lasse** mir die Haare **schneiden**.* 
  (I'm having my hair cut. / I let someone cut my hair.)
* *Er **lässt** sein Auto **reparieren**.* 
  (He is having his car repaired.)

### Function 3: Nicht tun / Aufhören (To stop doing something / not do it)
Used to tell someone to stop an action, or to leave a state as it is.
* *Lass das!* (Stop that! / Leave that!)
* *Wir **lassen** das Fenster **offen**.* (We are leaving the window open.)

---

## Part 2: Grammar & Conjugation

### 2.1 Present Tense Conjugation
*lassen* is a strong verb with a vowel change (a → ä) in the *du* and *er/sie/es* forms.

| Person | Form |
|---|---|
| ich | lasse |
| du | **lässt** |
| er/sie/es | **lässt** |
| wir | lassen |
| ihr | lasst |
| sie/Sie | lassen |

*(Note: du lässt and er lässt sound identical!)*

### 2.2 Sentence Structure (Präsens)
When used with another verb, *lassen* acts exactly like a Modal Verb. It takes Position 2, and the second verb goes to the **very end** as an Infinitiv.

| Position 1 | Position 2 (lassen) | Mittelfeld | Ende (Infinitiv) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ich | **lasse** | mein Auto in der Werkstatt | **reparieren**. |
| Der Vater | **lässt** | das Kind nicht fernsehen | **gucken**. |

### 2.3 The Perfekt Tense Trap: "gelassen" vs. "lassen"

This is where B1 students often make mistakes. The Perfekt form depends on how *lassen* is used in the sentence!

**Rule 1: "lassen" used ALONE (as a main verb)**
→ Perfekt = hat ... **gelassen**
* *Präsens:* Ich lasse meine Tasche hier. (I leave my bag here.)
* *Perfekt:* Ich habe meine Tasche hier **gelassen**.

**Rule 2: "lassen" used WITH ANOTHER VERB (Modal-like)**
→ Perfekt = hat ... Infinitiv + **lassen** (Double Infinitive!)
* *Präsens:* Ich lasse das Auto reparieren.
* *Perfekt:* Ich habe das Auto reparieren **lassen**. (NOT reparieren gelassen!)

## 3 · 📚 Expert Addition: Diagnostic Table

How do you know what a sentence with *lassen* means? Look at the context!

| German Sentence | Context | Function | 🇻🇳 Translation |
|---|---|---|---|
| *Der Lehrer lässt die Schüler früher gehen.* | School | **Allow** | Thầy giáo cho phép học sinh về sớm. |
| *Ich lasse mein Fahrrad reparieren.* | Broken thing | **Service** | Tôi đem xe đạp đi sửa (nhờ người khác sửa). |
| *Lass den Hund in Ruhe!* | Command | **Stop** | Để con chó yên! |
| *Er lässt den Kaffee auf dem Tisch stehen.* | Object location | **Leave state** | Anh ấy để ly cà phê trên bàn. |

### Contrast: Tự làm vs. Nhờ người làm (Do it yourself vs. Service)

| Self (Tự làm) | Service (Dùng dịch vụ với "lassen") |
|---|---|
| Ich **schneide** mir die Haare. (I cut my own hair.) | Ich **lasse** mir die Haare **schneiden**. |
| Ich **repariere** mein Auto. (I fix it myself.) | Ich **lasse** mein Auto **reparieren**. |
| Ich **putze** die Fenster. (I clean them myself.) | Ich **lasse** die Fenster **putzen**. |

## 4 · Common Mistakes (Häufige Fehler)

| # | ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Er laßt das Auto reparieren. | Er **lässt** das Auto reparieren. | Vowel change! a → ä |
| 2 | Ich habe das Auto reparieren gelassen. | Ich habe das Auto reparieren **lassen**. | When used with another verb, the Perfekt uses the Infinitiv "lassen" at the end (Double Infinitive). |
| 3 | Ich schneide meine Haare beim Friseur. | Ich **lasse** mir die Haare beim Friseur **schneiden**. | Unless you grab the scissors and cut it yourself at the salon, you must use "lassen"! |
