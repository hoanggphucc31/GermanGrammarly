# Module 11 — Flashcards: Das Verb "lassen"

## Forms & Conjugation
| Front | Back |
|---|---|
| Conjugate "lassen" for **du** and **er** (Present Tense) | du **lässt**, er **lässt** (Vowel change a -> ä) |
| Perfekt of "lassen" when used **ALONE** (I left my bag here) | Ich **habe** meine Tasche hier **gelassen**. |
| Perfekt of "lassen" with **ANOTHER VERB** (I had my car repaired) | Ich **habe** mein Auto reparieren **lassen**. (Double infinitive!) |

## Meanings
| Front | Back |
|---|---|
| Translate: "I am having my hair cut." | Ich **lasse** mir die Haare **schneiden**. |
| Translate: "Leave me alone!" | **Lass** mich in Ruhe! |
| Translate: "Stop that!" | **Lass** das! |

## Diagnostics
| Front | Back |
|---|---|
| What is the function of *lassen*? "Die Mutter lässt das Kind fernsehen." | **Erlauben** (Allowing) |
| What is the function of *lassen*? "Er lässt sein Fahrrad reparieren." | **Veranlassen** (Service / Having something done) |
| Why is this wrong? "Ich schneide meine Haare beim Friseur." | You must use **lassen** (Ich lasse mir die Haare schneiden), because you don't cut them yourself! |
