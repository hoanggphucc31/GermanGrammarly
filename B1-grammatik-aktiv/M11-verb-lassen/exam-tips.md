# Module 11 — Exam Tips: Das Verb "lassen"

## Goethe Zertifikat B1 Strategy

### 1. Schreiben (Writing Part 1 & 3)

* **Writing about services (Part 1):** You often have to write to a friend about something new (e.g., you moved to a new apartment, you bought a broken car). This is the perfect place to use *lassen*.
    * *Ich habe eine alte Wohnung gekauft, aber ich muss sie noch **renovieren lassen**.* (I bought an old apartment, but I still have to get it renovated.)
    * *Mein Handy war kaputt, also habe ich es **reparieren lassen**.* (My phone was broken, so I had it repaired.)
* **The "I cut my hair" mistake:** If you write *Ich habe meine Haare geschnitten* (I cut my hair), examiners will notice. Native speakers almost always use *lassen* here: *Ich habe mir die Haare **schneiden lassen**.*

### 2. Sprechen (Speaking Part 1 & 2)

* **Planning something (Part 1):** When planning an event with your partner, suggest having food delivered instead of cooking.
    * *Wir haben keine Zeit zum Kochen. Wir könnten uns Pizza **bringen lassen**.* (We don't have time to cook. We could have pizza delivered.)
* **Presentation (Part 2):** When talking about modern life, you can mention how people rely on services.
    * *Heutzutage haben die Leute wenig Zeit. Viele **lassen** ihre Kinder von einer Nanny **betreuen**.* (Nowadays people have little time. Many have their children cared for by a nanny.)

### 3. Lesen (Reading)

* **Careful with "lassen" in news articles (Teil 2):** In formal texts, *lassen* is often used as a passive alternative.
    * *Das Problem **lässt** sich leicht **lösen**.* = Das Problem kann leicht gelöst werden. (The problem can be easily solved.)
    * *Der Vertrag **lässt** sich nicht **ändern**.* = Der Vertrag kann nicht geändert werden. (The contract cannot be changed.)
    * -> If you see **sich lassen + Infinitiv**, it means "can be done".

### 4. Grammar Trap Reminder
Never use `gelassen` in the Perfekt if there is another verb in the sentence!
❌ Ich habe mein Fahrrad reparieren gelassen.
✅ Ich habe mein Fahrrad reparieren **lassen**.
