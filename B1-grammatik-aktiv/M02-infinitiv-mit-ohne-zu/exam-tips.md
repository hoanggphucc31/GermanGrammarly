# Module 02 — Exam Tips: Infinitiv mit/ohne zu

> 🎯 **Goethe B1:** Infinitive constructions with `um...zu` appear in almost every **Schreiben** task. Mastering this structure is essential for expressing purpose and reason.

---

## How This Grammar Appears

| Section | What to Watch |
|---|---|
| **Schreiben** | Expressing purpose: "Ich lerne Deutsch, **um** in Deutschland **zu arbeiten**." |
| **Sprechen** | Talking about plans: "Ich habe vor, **zu reisen**." |
| **Lesen** | Understanding complex sentences with infinitive clauses. |
| **Hören** | Plans and intentions: "Ich versuche, mehr Sport **zu machen**." |

## Key Tips

1. **um...zu is Your Best Friend (Schreiben)**
   * Every B1 writing task asks WHY you do something.
   * *Ich lerne Deutsch, **um** bessere Chancen **zu haben**.*
   * *Ich spare Geld, **um** in Urlaub **zu fahren**.*
   * Use this structure 1-2 times per essay for guaranteed bonus points!

2. **Separable Verb Trap**
   * The most common mistake: writing `zu einkaufen` instead of `einzukaufen`.
   * Remember: zu goes INSIDE separable verbs!

3. **The Quick Test**
   * Can you replace the verb with a Modalverb? If yes → no zu needed.
   * *Ich **kann** schwimmen.* = no zu.
   * *Ich **versuche** zu schwimmen.* = zu needed!

4. **lassen in Daily Life**
   * *Ich lasse mir die Haare **schneiden**.* (I have my hair cut.)
   * This is very natural and earns points in Sprechen.

> 🇻🇳 *Mẹo: Trong bài Schreiben B1, hãy dùng "um...zu" ít nhất 1 lần. Ví dụ: "Ich schreibe Ihnen, um mich über... zu beschweren." hoặc "Ich möchte Sie bitten, mir zu helfen." Đây là cấu trúc chuẩn B1.*
