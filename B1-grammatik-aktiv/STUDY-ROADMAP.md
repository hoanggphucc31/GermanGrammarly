# B1 Study Roadmap — Grammatik Aktiv + Supplementary Grammar

> **Recommended pace:** 1-2 modules per week (6-8 hours total per week).

---

## Supplementary B1 Grammar (Current Modules)

These modules cover core B1 grammar topics essential for Goethe B1 certification. They complement the textbook with standard B1 instruction topics.

| Week | Module | Focus | Target After |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **M01** | Relativsätze | Can combine sentences, describe in detail |
| 2 | **M02** | Infinitiv mit/ohne zu | Can express purpose, use infinitive constructions |
| 3 | **M03** | als & wenn | Can tell stories, describe conditions |
| 4 | **M04** | Modalpartikeln + Zeitadverbien | Can sound natural, express time nuances |

## Study Tips

1. **Start with `lesson.md`** — Read the grammar explanation carefully.
2. **Do `exercises.md`** — Write answers before checking.
3. **Check `answers.md`** — Understand WHY each answer is correct.
4. **Review `vocabulary.md`** — Learn the key words for this module.
5. **Use `flashcards.md`** — Daily 10-minute review.
6. **Read `exam-tips.md`** — Before any practice test.
