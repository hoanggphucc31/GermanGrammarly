# B1 Study Roadmap — Grammatik Aktiv

> **Recommended pace:** 1-2 modules per week.

---

## 📚 Supplementary B1 Grammar (Weeks 1-3)

These modules cover core B1 grammar topics essential for Goethe B1 certification. 

| Week | Module | Focus | Target After |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **M01** | Relativsätze | Can combine sentences, describe in detail |
| 2 | **M02** | Infinitiv mit/ohne zu | Can express purpose, use infinitive constructions |
| 3 | **M03/M04** | als/wenn & Partikeln | Can tell stories, sound natural in conversation |

---

## 📘 Phase 1: Advanced Verb Structures (Weeks 4-6)

These modules align directly with Kapitel 49-54 of the textbook.

| Week | Module | Focus | Target After |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | **M05** | Präteritum & Plusquamperfekt | Can narrate past events chronologically |
| 5 | **M06** | Reflexiv Dativ & Dual Verbs | Can use Dativ reflexive, identify inseparable prefixes |
| 6 | **M07** | Verb + Präposition | Can use fixed prepositions naturally, use darauf/worauf |

---

## 📘 Phase 2 & 3: Konjunktiv II, Passiv, Futur I (Weeks 7-9)

These modules align directly with Kapitel 55-60 of the textbook.

| Week | Module | Focus | Target After |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7 | **M08** | Konjunktiv II (Präsens/Vergangenheit) | Can express wishes, advice, and hypothetical situations |
| 8 | **M09** | Passiv (Präsens/Präteritum/Perfekt) | Can use passive voice for objective descriptions |
| 9 | **M10** | Futur I & werden (3 Funktionen) | Can make predictions and distinguish the 3 functions of werden |

## 📘 Phase 4: Final B1 Topics (Weeks 10-12)

These modules align directly with Kapitel 61-65 of the textbook.

| Week | Module | Focus | Target After |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | **M11** | Das Verb "lassen" | Can express getting a service done or allowing actions |
| 11 | **M12** | Positionsverben & Genitiv | Can distinguish location vs movement, use Genitiv |
| 12 | **M13** | N-Deklination & Adjektive | Can use advanced nouns and adjective-nouns correctly |

## 📘 Phase 5: Komplexe Sätze & Adjektive (Weeks 13-14)

These modules align with Kapitel 66-67 of the extended textbook.

| Week | Module | Focus | Target After |
|---|---|---|---|
| 13 | **M14** | Adjektive als neutrale Nomen | Can express abstract concepts (alles Gute, etwas Neues) |
| 14 | **M15** | Indirekte Fragen | Can ask polite questions embedded in sentences |
| 15 | **M16** | Temporale Nebensätze 2 | Can sequence events using während, bevor, nachdem, seit |
| 16 | **M17** | Finalsätze, Doppelkonnektoren, Vergleichssätze | Can express purpose (damit), use double connectors, je...desto |
| 17 | **M18** | Präpositionen 2 | Can use Genitiv prepositions and expanded temporal prepositions |
| 18 | **M19** | Adjektive & Partizipien | Can use Nullartikel adjective endings and Partizip I as adjectives |

## Study Tips

1. **Start with `lesson.md`** — Read the grammar explanation carefully.
2. **Do `exercises.md`** — Write answers before checking.
3. **Check `answers.md`** — Understand WHY each answer is correct.
4. **Review `vocabulary.md`** — Learn the key words for this module.
5. **Use `flashcards.md`** — Daily 10-minute review.
6. **Read `exam-tips.md`** — Before any practice test.
