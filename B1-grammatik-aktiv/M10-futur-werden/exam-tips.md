# Module 10 — Exam Tips: Futur I & `werden`

## Goethe Zertifikat B1 Strategy

### 1. Sprechen (Speaking Part 1 & 2)

* **Making Promises:** In Part 1, you often have to organize something with your partner. Promising to do a task is a great place to use Futur I.
    * *Kein Problem, ich **werde** mich um die Getränke **kümmern**.* (No problem, I will take care of the drinks.)
* **Making Assumptions:** If you are discussing a topic and aren't sure about a fact, use `wird wohl`. It sounds extremely natural.
    * *Der Kurs **wird wohl** zu teuer **sein**, oder?* (The course will probably be too expensive, right?)

### 2. Schreiben (Writing Part 1 & 3)

* **Apologizing and Promising (Part 3):** When writing a formal apology for missing an appointment, you should promise that it won't happen again.
    * *Es tut mir sehr leid. Das **wird** nicht wieder **passieren**.* (I am very sorry. That will not happen again.)
* **Predicting (Part 1):** When writing to a friend about a future event.
    * *Ich bin sicher, die Party **wird** super **werden**!* (I am sure the party will be great! -> First "wird" is Futur I, second "werden" is the main verb "become".)

### 3. Lesen (Reading) & Hören (Listening)

* **The Timeline Trap:** The B1 exam loves to test if you know WHEN something happens. If a text says *Das Museum wird renoviert* (Passiv Präsens), it is happening NOW. If it says *Das Museum wird renovieren* (Futur I Active - grammatically weird but possible if the museum is acting as an entity) or *Das Museum wird renoviert werden* (Futur I Passiv - C1 level!), it's the future.
* ALWAYS look at the end of the sentence to see if it's an Infinitiv (Future) or Partizip II (Passive)!

### 4. General Tip: Don't overuse Futur I
In spoken German, if you are just stating a plan ("I am flying to Berlin tomorrow"), use Präsens + time word (*Ich fliege morgen nach Berlin*). Futur I (*Ich werde morgen nach Berlin fliegen*) is grammatically correct but sounds unnecessarily formal or dramatic. Save Futur I for predictions, promises, and assumptions.
