## Conjunctions for Time Sequence
| German | 🇻🇳 | Function |
|---|---|---|
| **nachdem** | sau khi | Requires Plusquamperfekt for past events (Action 1) |
| **bevor** | trước khi | Takes Präteritum/Perfekt (Action 2) |
| **dann / danach** | sau đó | Linking adverbs (Position 1) |
